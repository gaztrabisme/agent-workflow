# Unit 4 · Finishing and Sharing Your Work

Anchor: 3069 · U4

You are nearly finished! Now is the time to get some feedback and check\-ups to see if the visualization needs some edits. Once this is done, it's time to share your work!

## Tasks

- ### Get Feedback 
Practice exercise 4\.1

Ask someone to have a look at your visualization. Without explaining, see if they understand the directions, the legend, the connections, and so on. Have them point out what they like, what they don’t like, and why.


![](https://assets9.domestika.org/course-images/000/028/629/28629-big.jpg)
- ### Test Readability

It's generally a good idea to check if your design is colourblind proof. Do this in Adobe Illustrator or search for a suitable online tool. If there is not enough contrast, you might want to change your colours.

Zooming out might also help see if there is a good balance of colours and if the elements you want to emphasize are popping out enough. Another way to check is by looking through your eyelashes.
- ### Print Your Poster

It's time to print out your poster. There are endless online print services to choose from, but of course, you can also go to your local print service!


![](https://assets0.domestika.org/course-images/000/028/630/28630-big.jpg)
- ### Present Your Work Online

Let your audience know that you have been creating a new project. Post your sketches, work in progress, or experiments that you have been working on. Then, using your website, social media, or portfolio, share your final result as well!


![](https://assets1.domestika.org/course-images/000/028/631/28631-big.jpg)
