Hi and welcome to my course on Domestika! I'm Sonja Kuijpers, running the one\-woman information design company Studio Terp based in Eindhoven, Netherlands.

- ### Data Visualization Knowledge

If you are just getting started in the field of data visualization and would like to know more, there are lots of resources out there to learn about the basics, related subjects, and history. Here are some websites and books worth mentioning.

**Websites**

[Data Viz Project](https://datavizproject.com/)
  
A website trying to present all relevant data visualizations so you can find the right visualization and get inspired on how to make it.

[Nightingale](https://nightingaledvs.com/)
  
The journal of the Data Visualization Society focuses on data visualization from personal stories to exploratory research to interviews with leaders in the community on data ethics and best practices.

**Books**

[*The Functional Art: An Introduction to Information Graphics and Visualization* by Alberto Cairo](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/13705587-the-functional-art)
  
[*Data Visualisation: A Handbook for Data Driven Design* by Andy Kirk](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/43586678-data-visualisation)
  
[*Storytelling with Data: A Data Visualization Guide for Business Professional*s by Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/26535513-storytelling-with-data)
  
[*The Wall Street Journal Guide to Information Graphics: The Dos and Don'ts of Presenting Data, Facts, and Figures* by Dona M. Wong](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/6542897-the-wall-street-journal-guide-to-information-graphics)
  
[*Better Data Visualizations: A Guide for Scholars, Researchers, and Wonks* by Jonathan Schwabish](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/53214552-better-data-visualizations)
  
[Data visualization handbook by Juuso Koponen and Jonatan Hildén](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/52326089-data-visualization-handbook)
  
[*Dear Data* by Giorgia Lupi and Stefanie Posavec](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/29973489-dear-data)
  
[*Invisible Women: Data Bias in a World Designed for Men* by Caroline Criado Pérez](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/41104077-invisible-women)
  
[*Naked Statistics: Stripping the Dread from the Data* by Charles Wheelan](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/17986418-naked-statistics)
  
[*Everydata: The Misinformation Hidden in the Little Data You Consume Every Day* by John H. Johnson and Mike Gluck](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/25898794-everydata?ac=1&from_search=true&qid=WowjIMjQ1d&rank=1)
