## Start with a subject that moves you

Anchor: 3069 · U2 · L03 · 00:00–01:12

Effective Data Visualization: Transform Information into Art · U02 · L03 · 00:00–01:12

In this lesson I’ll talk about how to find a subject. It can be hard to find something that relates to you, and I find it helpful to connect the subject to your own emotions and interests. Think about the subjects that inspire or move you. What makes you sad or happy, angry or irritated? What else makes you feel strongly?

Think about things you have good memories of. Look for books, movies, or other things that inspired you when you were young or inspire you now. You can also revisit books or movies that feel nostalgic and see whether they contain data for a visualization.

You can begin looking online, on your bookshelves, or elsewhere in your home. Look for something you can pull out and show to other people. There is data all around you. In this course the final project will be a poster based on data harvested from a book, rather than a database found online. In a book, we can look for characters, grammar, the storyline, the role of time, and named locations.
