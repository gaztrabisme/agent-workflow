## Move back and forth between tools and ideas

Anchor: 3069 · U3 · L09 · 00:00–01:37

3069 · U03 · L09 · 00:00–01:37
My workflow is not linear. I often go back and forth between programs and return to the source, which in this course is the book. I sketch in between, but I am not very organized: the sketches end up on sticky notes, on the backs of printouts, or in a notebook.

When you need more information, return to Google or another search engine. You can also go into Excel to see what you can do with the numbers and try different charts to see whether they show something you want to present. You can do this in RAWGraphs [captions: “Wargrass”] or another tool you prefer.

Do not worry too much about making mistakes. They are part of the process and can lead to fun or to discovering another shape or colour you can use. Treat this as exploration and learning rather than failure. Take your time and pause when needed; there is no rush [captions: “wash”].
