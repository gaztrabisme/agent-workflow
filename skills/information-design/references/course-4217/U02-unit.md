# Unit 2 · The Design of Visual Narratives

Anchor: 4217 · U2

In this unit, I will talk about data visualization and visual narratives. I will start by introducing the field, before tackling the role of perception and the questions to ask yourself before starting a project. 

After I will discuss the process of telling stories with data, visual words, and visual metaphors, while analyzing some examples and case studies.

## Additional resources

- ### Data Visualization Books I Recommend

The art of effective data visualization comes from uncovering the hidden stories within vast amounts of data and finding ways to share those stories with the world in the form of charts, maps, and infographics. 

If you want to master the skill of working with data in order to humanize it and make it more accessible, I suggest starting with the following foundational guides. Don't forget to note down any quotes that strike a chord with you. 

[*Data Feminism* \| Catherine D'Ignazio and Lauren F. Klein](https://mitpress.mit.edu/books/data-feminism)
  
[*The Truthful Art: Data, Charts, and Maps for Communication* \| Alberto Cairo](https://www.peachpit.com/store/truthful-art-data-charts-and-maps-for-communication-9780321934079)
  
[*Data Visualisation: A Handbook for Data Driven Design* \| Andy Kirk](https://uk.sagepub.com/en-gb/eur/data-visualisation/book266150)
  
[*­Building Science Graphics: An illustrated guide to communicating science through diagrams and visualizations* \| Jen Christiansen](https://www.buildingsciencegraphics.com/)
  
[*W. E. B. Du Bois's Data Portraits: Visualizing Black America* \| Britt Rusert and Whitney Battle\-Baptiste](https://papress.com/products/w-e-b-du-boiss-data-portraits-visualizing-black-america?_pos=1&_sid=bd3aa8efb&_ss=r)
  
["Dataviz Books Everyone Should Read"](https://informationisbeautiful.net/visualizations/dataviz-books/)
- ### Useful Websites

There are endless treasure coves of data\-related tools on the web. Here are some key websites I use to find inspiration from other talented professionals in this field. If you're stuck on finding a topic to choose, this is a great place to start.

[Data Viz Project](https://datavizproject.com/)
  
This website presents a catalog of data visualizations, to help find the right visualization and get inspired on how to make them.

[Datawrapper Blog](https://blog.datawrapper.de/)
  
A blog by people who work at Datawrapper, an online tool to make charts. It's full of excellent and thoughtful posts on data visualization.

[*Nightingale Magazine*](https://nightingaledvs.com/)
  
The journal of the Data Visualization Society—an organization dedicated to fostering a community for data visualization professionals. It publishes articles on current issues in data visualization for professionals and a general audience.

[FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/)
  
A data visualization blog curated by Nathan Yau. It explores how we use analysis and visualization to understand data and ourselves.

[Color Oracle. Design for the Color Impaired](https://colororacle.org/)
  
A free color blindness simulator.

[*What to Consider When Choosing Colors for Data Visualization* \| Lisa Charlotte Muth](https://academy.datawrapper.de/article/140-what-to-consider-when-choosing-colors-for-data-visualization)
  
[*Does Data Visualization Have Rules? Or Is It All Just "It Depends"?* \| Nick Desbarats](https://nightingaledvs.com/does-data-visualization-have-rules-or-is-it-all-just-it-depends/)
