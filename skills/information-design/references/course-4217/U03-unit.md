# Unit 3 · The Data

Anchor: 4217 · U3

In this unit, I will work on the contents of the piece—the data that will create the story. I will show you where and how to find this, starting from your personal interests. Then, you will learn how to create a dataset using Google Sheets. Finally, you will start working on the macrostructure of the storytelling piece.

## Tasks

- ### Explore and Find a Topic

I've shown you the sources I will use for my course project and the topic I'm interested in communicating. Now you can select yours! You can decide whether to choose the same topic I'm showing or to explore a subject you are particularly curious or passionate about. I recommend finding a story that you would love to tell.
- ### Choose Your Data and Download It

Now that you have selected a topic, choose the datasets you will use and download them. In the Additional Resources section, you will find the links to download the ones that I will use, but feel free to choose whatever data you want.


![](https://assets4.domestika.org/course-images/000/042/934/42934-big.jpg)
- ### Create Your Dataset on Google Sheets

Now you can create your own dataset on Google Sheets. 


![](https://assets3.domestika.org/course-images/000/042/933/42933-big.jpg)
- ### Start Sketching the Structure of the Storytelling Piece 
Practice exercise 3\.1

Once you've created your dataset, you can start sketching the structure of your storytelling piece. Remember to think about the order in which the readers are going to see the data. 

Then, draw some drafts of the visual variables that you will use to visualize the information.
