# Unit 4 · Designing the Visualization

Anchor: 4217 · U4

In this unit, I will teach you all the steps that will allow you to create a visual narrative. You will learn how to combine RAWGraphs and Adobe Illustrator to create a data visualization. 

I will also showcase some of the tricks and tools I use to design both accurate and experimental pieces that aim to engage the reader. Then, I will guide you in designing the legend. To finish, I will talk about outputs—these will change depending on the devices that you want the design to work for.

## Tasks

- ### Explore RAWGraphs

Open RAWGraphs and select the visual model you want for your project. 

Start exploring the tools and try to create a few options from the same dataset. This can be the dataset you choose for the course project or, as a practice, you can use a table from RAWGraphs' own data samples. This is a good way to start exploring how different visual representations can tell different stories.
- ### Look for Visual Inspiration

Create a collection of images that you will use as a reference. These can be existing data visualization projects or images from other worlds (like art, graphic design, nature, or illustration). 

Select images that attract your gaze and try to find the elements that you would like to replicate, such as a specific color, texture, or shape. This can help you to find your personal approach to designing visual alphabets.

As you move forward with your project and the exercises, save images of your process. These step\-by\-step images will be needed for your presentation in the "Final Project" unit, which will also become part of your Domestika portfolio!
- ### Use RAWGraphs for Your Graphic

Now that you've explored the tool, use RAWGraphs to create your visualization, or, as in my example, a part of it.


![](https://assets6.domestika.org/course-images/000/042/956/42956-big.jpg)
- ### Start Working in Adobe Illustrator

Now, work on the graphic using Adobe Illustrator. Follow the steps I laid out in "Adobe Illustrator: Creating the Visualization" to add new layers of information to your storytelling piece.

Document your progress by capturing step\-by\-step images as you go and note down any thoughts you have about the decisions you make. That way, when you share your project, I can tune into your creative process and provide better feedback. 


![](https://assets5.domestika.org/course-images/000/042/955/42955-big.jpg)
- ### Work on the Details in Adobe Illustrator

Working on the details is an important part of my process. I like to adopt a careful and thoughtful approach. Dedicate some time to perfect your project and curate the visual refinements.


![](https://assets3.domestika.org/course-images/000/042/953/42953-big.jpg)
- ### Design the Legend and Write the Explanatory Text 
Practice exercise 4\.1

Work on all those elements that will help you to create a bridge between you and the readers: the legend and the explanatory text. Don't forget to keep your text short and clear! 

Both I and the other students will be there to comment on your work and give useful feedback.


![](https://assets2.domestika.org/course-images/000/042/952/42952-big.jpg)

Once you've finalized your project, head to the next unit titled "Final Project" and share it! There you will find detailed instructions on how to do so. I'll be on hand to answer any questions and give feedback once it's uploaded. 

Sharing is important. Getting feedback and support is an essential step: it can keep you motivated and help you to observe your work in a new light. Domestika is a great community for artists. There is no place for negativity, only encouragement!
