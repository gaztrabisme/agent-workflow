# Unit 1 · Introduction

Anchor: 4217 · U1

Hello! Welcome to this Domestika course and thank you for joining me. I'm Federica Fragapane, a freelance designer in the field of data visualization. I've worked for various organizations, companies, and magazines.

In this unit, I will introduce myself and my path. You will learn why I entered this field and the influences that had a significant impact on my approach.

## Tasks

- ### Share Your Influences 
Practice exercise 1\.2

I shared with you the fields that have inspired me. Now it's time to share with me your own references and passions. 

What are the worlds that have influenced or could influence your approach to data visualization design? Thinking about your personal inclinations will be a great way to get started if you're new to this field.

- ### My Influences

I am a passionate communicator. All of my inspirations come from my love and desire to communicate. To achieve this, I like to use all the tools I have: shapes, colors, and words. As a result, my influences are spread across many different fields, like literature, art, and design. 

In the lesson "Influences", I referred to some inspirational quotes that have stayed with me. If you'd like to delve into these further, I have linked the relevant works and artists below:

[*Design as Art* \| Bruno Munari](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/19371/design-as-art-by-bruno-munari/9780141035819)
  
A book written by Bruno Munari I particularly love.

["Bruno Munari"](https://www.domusweb.it/en/biographies/bruno-munari.html)
  
More on the work of Munari, Italian artist and designer.

[*Data Feminism* \| Catherine D'Ignazio and Lauren F. Klein](https://mitpress.mit.edu/books/data-feminism)
  
An important and illuminating book recommended for anyone who wants to work with data.

["Alessandro Mendini"](https://www.domusweb.it/en/biographies/alessandro-mendini.html)
  
On the work of Alessandro Mendini, Italian designer, architect, and artist.
