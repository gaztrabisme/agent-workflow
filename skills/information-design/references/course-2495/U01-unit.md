# Unit 1 · Introduction

Anchor: 2495 · U1

Hello! Welcome to my course! In this unit, I will introduce myself and I will tell you how I got into the data visualization field. I will also share with you the influences that had an impact in my approach to design.

## Tasks

- ### Share Your Influences 
Practice exercise 1\.2

I shared with you my influences, and as you have seen, they come from very different domains. What are your influences, and who inspires you? Share your influences, your passions, both from data visualization and from other fields.

- ### Mark Lombardy

[Mark Lombardi at MoMA](https://www.moma.org/artists/22980)
  
Mark Lombardy’s work inspired me when I learned about him at university. He was an American neo\-conceptual artist who specialized in drawings that document "the uses and abuses of power."
- ### Alice Rawsthorn

[*Design as an Attitude*](http://alicerawsthorn.com/books.php)
  
This is a book I often come back to. Alice Rawsthorn is a design critic and author. Her design projects and approaches to design deeply inspire me.
- ### Else Bostelmann

["These women unlocked the mysteries of the deep sea"](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/these-women-unlocked-the-mysteries-of-the-deep-sea)
  
Else Bostelmann is an artist who joined the New York Zoological Society in 1929 to paint marine life. She also joined William Beebe's bathysphere oceanographic expeditions at Nonsuch Island, in Bermuda. I love her paintings, and her art inspires me.
- ### Accurat Studio

[Accurat](https://accuratstudio.medium.com/)
  
Working at Accurate was an extremely inspiring experience. It helped me a lot in growing professionally and personally. This is their Medium profile, where they share a behind\-the\-scenes look at their projects and some interesting articles about data visualization.
