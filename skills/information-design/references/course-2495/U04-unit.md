# Unit 4 · Visualizing the Data

Anchor: 2495 · U4

In this unit, I will guide you in all the steps to visualize the data we have selected. We will design a static data visualization with multiple levels of information. We’ll keep an eye on the visual experimentation \- as a way to create an engaging piece \- and for this reason, I will show you how I look for visual inspiration from worlds that are not only related to data visualization. We will then design our visualization using RAWGraphs and Adobe Illustrator. I will show you the process and some tricks I use. We’ll then work on the legend and the explanatory text: those elements are essential to create a piece able to talk to the readers. Finally, we’ll spend some time visually refining our project, curating it in detail.

## Tasks

- ### Look For Visual Inspiration

Look for visual inspiration and select the images your eyes find beautiful and inspiring.
- ### Create a Collection of Images

Create a collection with the images you gathered images, and divide them into two categories: those that are for data visualization projects that you like and images coming from other fields and domains (art, nature, sculpture, fields you are passionate about). It will help you to find your personal visual language.
- ### Use the Images to Define the Visual Model

Now that you have selected the images that inspire you, use them to sketch your own visual model, keeping in mind your data structure.
- ### Use RAWGraphs to Design the Structure of Your Graphic

Transfer the dataset onto RAWGraphs and work on it to design the main structure of your visualization.
- ### Take It to Adobe Illustrator and Work on It

Now you can work on the graphic using Adobe Illustrator. Follow the steps I laid out in this unit to add new layers of information to the main structure.

![](https://assets0.domestika.org/course-images/000/020/350/20350-big.jpg)
- ### Design the Legend and Write the Explanatory Text 
Practice exercise 4\.1

After you picked the visual rules your graphic must follow, create the legend and write a short text explaining everything.
- ### Test Your Project 
Practice exercise 4\.2

Time to test your visualization. Show it to people who are not data visualization experts to see how do they respond to it.
- ### Finalize and Share Your Project 
Practice exercise 4\.3

Work on the feedback that you have received and take some time to work on the refinements and details.
