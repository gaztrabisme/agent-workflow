# Unit 2 · About Data Visualization

Anchor: 2495 · U2

In this unit, I’m going to talk about the broad field of data visualization. I’m going to introduce the concept, talking about its role and potential. We’ll explore the visual variables that we can use to give shape to numbers and stories. Finally, I’m going to talk about an essential relationship: the one between designer and readers.

## Tasks

- ### Read, Listen and Watch the Additional Resources to Get Inspired

You can read the most recent [blog posts](http://www.thefunctionalart.com/) written by Alberto Cairo, listen to an episode of [Andy Kirk’s podcast](https://www.visualisingdata.com/2020/12/the-complete-season-2-of-explore-explain/), check FlowingData’s [visualization guides](https://flowingdata.com/category/guides/) or watch one talk from [Eyeo Festival](https://vimeo.com/eyeofestival) that you think can inspire you!

## Additional resources

- ### Alberto Cairo

[Alberto Cairo](http://albertocairo.com/)
  
This is Alberto Cairo’s website full of information about design and data visualization.
- ### Andy Kirk

[Andy Kirk](https://www.visualisingdata.com/)
  
Andy Kirk’s website about data visualization. You will find resources such as “Best of the visualization web”, a monthly curated list of data visualization projects, and “Explore Explain”, a long\-form video and podcast series on data visualization.
- ### Data in Flow

[FlowingData](https://flowingdata.com/)
  
FlowingData is a data visualization blog curated by Nathan Yau. It explores how we use analysis and visualization to understand data and ourselves.
- ### Must\-Read Books on Dataviz

["Dataviz books everyone should read"](https://informationisbeautiful.net/visualizations/dataviz-books/)
  
This is a curated selection of data visualization books.
- ### All Sorts of Data Visualizations

[Data Viz Project](https://datavizproject.com/)
  
This is a website that features a huge, varied assortment of data visualizations. Data Viz Project is a great place to look for inspiration.
- ### Eyeo Festival’s Talks

[Eyeo Festival's videos at Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/eyeofestival)
  
The inspiring talks that you can find at Eyeo Festival's Vimeo channel are worth your time. They are truly captivating. Eyeo Festival is an event that brings together creative coders, data designers and all kinds of creators working at the intersection of data, art and technology.
- ### A Journal for the DataViz Community

[*Nightingale*](https://medium.com/nightingale)
  
Take a look at the journal of the Data Visualization Society, which is an organization dedicated to fostering a community for data visualization professionals. It publishes articles on current issues in data visualization for professionals and a general audience alike.
- ### The Shape of Dreams

["Visualizing the Shape of Dreams for Google Trends"](https://medium.com/@frcfr/visualizing-the-shape-of-dreams-for-google-trends-5164c3c4e382)
  
In this Medium post, I talk about the design process of a project commissioned by Google Trends in which we tried to visualize dream patterns.
