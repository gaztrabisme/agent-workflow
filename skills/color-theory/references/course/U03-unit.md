# Unit 3 · Illustration color (50 colors)

Anchor: 1140 · U3

In this unit, we will develop an illustration with colored plates, limiting our resources to get closer and closer to the essence of an illustration. Through these exercises, we will see how to develop a color palette from scratch, to create harmony, rhythm and balance in the image.

## Tasks

- ### Analyze 5 of your own illustrations and ask yourself why they don't work

With what you have learned in this unit, do a self\-criticism of your 5 best illustrations. Write (it is very important, that you don't just think about it, write it!) A small note for each one where you explain to yourself if what is missing or can be improved is balance, harmony, history or any other of the items you explain in the unit.
- ### Add story elements to 2 existing illustrations 
Practice exercise 3\.1

Choose 2 of your images and add different elements to enrich its story, and thus improve its quality. If you don't know what to add, follow the process of defining the story of an image seen in this unit, and describe what your illustration tells, who the protagonists are, what they do, etc ... 

 Share your illustrations along with the story they tell on the forum. Comment and receive comments from your colleagues.
- ### Make 5 abstract compositions in black and white applying the Yin Yang principle 
Practice exercise 3\.2

Create 5 compositions in black and white, without looking for them to represent something. The goal of this exercise is to train your sensitivity. It's a bit complex, but just ask yourself when you FEEL that the composition works best while making adjustments (either because you moved such a shape or darkened such a part). Trust your instincts! 

 Share images of these compositions on the forum to receive feedback.
- ### Make 5 abstract color compositions applying the Yin Yang principle 
Practice exercise 3\.3

Trust your instincts and repeat the same exercise but this time using colors instead of black and white. Ask yourself when you FEEL that the composition works best. 

 Share what you consider your best image achieved in this forum.
- ### Get a sad mood, a dramatic one, and a very happy one through color.

Download the attached file that you will find below. Open it in Photoshop and modify the colors and values of the different layers to alter the cheerful feel of the image. 

 Create 3 images: one sad, one dramatic, and one happy, trying to change the original approach of the illustration. Add additional elements if necessary.
- ### Play to ruin the image

For this task, use the attached editable file and create two new images. 

 In the first one, find the point where you move one or another element of the image, you change the size of the elements, their color or their value but you do it in a way that still looks good. Seek to make strong modifications, do not make changes so subtle that they are barely noticeable. 

 In the second it looks for the point from which the image quality is fully compromised. So it already looks ugly.
- ### Create a color composition based on a narrative description

For this task, you must create your own illustration following the entire process seen in this unit.

 **Step 1:** Make a description of the story of your image, the moment you are representing, its protagonists and secondary elements.

 **Step 2:** Create the composition of your image with 3 values, to establish the main contrasts and the rhythm of the shapes.

 **Step 3:** Convert your values into colors. To choose your colors, remember to look for them on the color wheel and make sure they are separated enough to create contrast.

 **Step 4:** Create harmony by adding a third color.
- ### Find 25 images that you consider excellent in terms of balance, rhythm and harmony and copy them with 3 values

Like the previous task, in this one you will be able to work on your observation skills. Choose 25 images that you like a lot for their COMPOSITION and ILLUMINATION. Copy each one using only 3 values: a dark value, a light value, and a medium value. 

 The point of this exercise is to try to identify the large dark, light and medium blocks in the image, as you have seen in the lessons of this unit.
- ### Copy 3 images with spot colors 
Practice exercise 3\.4

Choose 3 images that you consider excellent in terms of balance, rhythm and harmony and copy them with flat colors (hard brush, no gradients). Try to limit the number of colors you use to a maximum of 50\. 

 Share the original and the copy in this forum with your classmates and see the images they shared. You can also give and receive feedback.
