## Additional resources

- ### My influences

[**Alex Mandrajev interview**](https://youtu.be/3ZFCcgSWjb8)
  
 One of the best artists of color for me. Here is an interview of him… in English, but with a live sample :) 

 [**Alla Prima Richard Schmid**](https://www.richardschmid.com/Alla-Prima-II-By-Richard-Schmid-p/skuap.htm)
  
 An indispensable book to learn about images themselves. The author is one of the greatest exponents of current painting.
- ### Studio binder

The guys at Studio Binder do a fantastic job breaking down movies for us. [In this post](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/how-to-use-color-in-film-50-examples-of-movie-color-palettes/), speak to us of color in movies and how they use it to create emotion.
- ### Movies in color

[An interesting site](https://moviesincolor.com/) to see films from another angle, that of color.
- ### Interview with Szymon Biernacki, Klaus Art Director (Netflix / Spa Studios)

I have known Szymon for several years and he is an amazing artist and a great human being. [In this series of interviews](https://youtu.be/PL4KCbRTsqc), shares his story, as well as his thoughts on frustration, personal growth, and work ethic.
- ### Study color with Trolls

[On my YouTube channel, Canvas,](https://youtu.be/0-sEuoH1vzI) I invite you to a challenge where we learn while creating. [In this case](https://youtu.be/ZN0VEaIMZz4), I show you techniques and exercises to study with the Trolls movie (Dreamworks) and learn about its fascinating color.
