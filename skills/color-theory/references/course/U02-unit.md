# Unit 2 · Color for concept (4 million colors)

Anchor: 1140 · U2

In this unit, we will talk about the narrative of color, how to identify the *mood* of an image and use it to create stories with image sequences. I will explain how light works, a necessary element for creating an effective narrative with color.

## Tasks

- ### Analyze why certain images make you react

Collect reference images that share the same *mood* and analyze why they make you feel that way. Write a short description of the images and the elements that are key in transmitting that feeling. Analyze lighting, contrast, color, and define how the artist used these three factors to convey emotion.
- ### Create images with different moods 
Practice exercise 2\.1

In the Attached Documents, you can download a PSD organized so that you can simply modify the different layers. In this task, I ask you to try to change the current *mood* of the image by modifying only its colors and values. 

 Try to convey a sense of terror, one of happiness, another of suspense, and finally of sterility (eg, think of a hospital, or a cold room). 

![](https://assets7.domestika.org/course-images/000/012/047/12047-big.jpg)
- ### Create a color script with existing movie images

Write the story of a little moment that could be a scene from a movie. For example, “The hero enters a room, it is very dark, in the background he sees someone sitting in a well of light. It comes closer and the lights come on. It's a trap. They start shooting everywhere. " 

 The important thing is that in the scene you imagine, there is an evolution that light and color can support. In my example, we go from darkness, to darkness with a well of light, to a fully lit scene, to shots that can be colorful ... 

 Now, take several screenshots of movies that have the *mood* that you imagine for each frame (image) of your scene, they can be of *live action* or animation. You can use the animationscreencaps.com site and screenmusings.com 

 Choose 5 of these images and place them in small bullets on the same line, as I did in lesson 2\. Thus, you will form a type of *color script.* Try to organize the images according to their color and values to tell your story.
- ### Create 3 different stories with the same images 
Practice exercise 2\.2

Taking up the *color script* created in the previous task, create 2 other little stories. Now try to count them with the same images but altering their order in the sequence. 

 Thus, you are training your ability to tell stories with images. In the following lessons, we will learn to create these images and control the color, but the first step is for you to see that you can already create your own stories with the images of others; it's what a director does. 

![](https://assets8.domestika.org/course-images/000/012/048/12048-big.jpg)
- ### Copy 4 color images trying to make them look as similar as possible 
Practice exercise 2\.3

Choose 4 images that seem wonderful to you in terms of color, light and values. Now open a file in Photoshop and try to copy these images as precisely as possible. Try searching for each shade until you find exactly the same one. It's a tough task, but don't quit and be very critical. I recommend that you do not use the dropper (pipette), do it only with pure observation. 

 The point is to train your eye to identify and reproduce colors as they are. You will see that you will be very surprised at first. If you prefer to do this exercise with watercolor, gouache or any other medium, go ahead. The point is to observe. 

![](https://assets9.domestika.org/course-images/000/012/049/12049-big.jpg)
- ### Copy 25 images with only 3 values 
Practice exercise 2\.4

Like the previous task, in this one you will be able to work on your observation skills. Choose 25 images that you like a lot for their COMPOSITION and ILLUMINATION. 

 Copy each one using only 3 values: a dark value, a light value, and a medium value. The point of this exercise is to try to identify the large dark, light and medium blocks in the image, as you have seen in the lessons in this unit. 

![](https://assets0.domestika.org/course-images/000/012/050/12050-big.jpg)
- ### Create your own lighting set

To understand color and light, it is not enough to observe them, you also have to experience them. For this task, gather various elements that you have in your environment and place them on your bed, on the floor, your desk, etc. 

 What matters is that you do a set of things that you like and that you arrange them in a way that you find beautiful. Then illuminate the objects with 3 different lights, in 3 different directions and take a photo for each one. If you don't have 3 different lights, use the light from your cell phone and just move it in 3 different positions so that the shadows are projected differently.
- ### Copy your scenes

Based on the photos you took in the previous task, copy the 3 compositions you created in color. Try to reproduce the colors and their respective values as closely as possible.
