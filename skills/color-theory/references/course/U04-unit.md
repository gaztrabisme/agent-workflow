# Unit 4 · Graphic color (10 colors)

Anchor: 1140 · U4

In this unit, we will talk about the importance of simplicity, about how using few colors forces us to make stronger decisions and gives identity to our images. Also, we will expose how important color is when defining a graphic style for your creations.

## Tasks

- ### Apply color theory!

Create 3 different palettes (analog, complementary and multicolor) with the editable file that you can download from the Additional Resources. Choose the main colors and harmonize with the secondary colors as you have seen in the lessons of the previous unit.
- ### Train your eye

Pick 5 random colors in a picture from your references. Then create 5 charts in a separate document (with a white background) where you are going to try to guess each of these 5 colors based only on your observation. 

 Once done, compare your color to the original with the color picker. Repeat this exercise 10 times with different images each time.
- ### The most difficult exercise

Open the drive's editable file, duplicate it, and convert the duplicate version to black and white. Now, create a Black and White adjustment layer in the original file. 

 The task is to try to modify the colors of the editable file without modifying the values. So you are going to try to turn yellows into pink for example but without modifying the value. It is more complicated than it seems since when modifying the color, the value is automatically modified (in fact, certain colors are darker and others lighter by nature), and you have to make some adjustments so that the value remains the same.
- ### Create your own personal identity 
Practice exercise 4\.1

Create a logo for yourself, as if you were a brand and choose no more than three colors to do it. Think that these colors are going to represent you. Then use these elements and this color palette to create a simple poster that represents you as well. 

 It doesn't matter that you've never made a logo or poster before, the important thing is the exercise itself and the results you can achieve with what you've learned in this unit. Trust your work and share it with your colleagues.
- ### Solve your image problems 
Practice exercise 4\.2

Go back to the 5 images that you improved in the second unit. Now, you are going to apply the advice I gave you to solve color problems. Do it with the 5 images. 

 First identify which problem we review belongs to, and then apply the technique to solve it. Post the before and after of each one in the forum.
- ### Copy 10 images with 15 colors 
Practice exercise 4\.3

Choose 3 images that you consider excellent in terms of balance, rhythm and harmony and copy them with flat colors (hard brush, no gradients). Try to limit the number of colors you use to a maximum of 10\. 

 Share your best copy with your peers on the forum.
- ### Develop contrasts 
Practice exercise 4\.4

Open the unit's editable file. For this task, you are going to create 3 different versions by modifying the color and the values. 

 The point of the exercise is to see how to move the focus of the image by playing with contrasts. Make a version modifying the contrast of values, another the saturation contrast, and another the color contrast. Decide where you want the attention to go and put the contrasts in this place. Try to make combinations to see how best you manage to direct the attention of your audience. 

 Ask the people around you what they see first to check that you achieved your mission, you can also post them in the forum to receive comments from your colleagues and give them your opinion on their images.
