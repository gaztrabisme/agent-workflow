## What you will learn: planning and choosing color

Anchor: 1140 · U1 · L03 · 00:22–01:02

What I am going to teach you in this course is how you can create color: set it up and plan it (*plantearlo, planearlo*) from the very beginning, so that it keeps adding to your image. I am going to give you a method for creating any kind of image and making sure that, with color, you reach a better result.

I will also teach you how to choose color: how to decide why this color rather than another, and how to create color compositions (*composiciones de color*), so that in the end you can decide which color composition, which palette, best suits the feeling, the story, the image and the moment you want to paint.

## Course structure: theory in reverse

Anchor: 1140 · U1 · L03 · 01:02–01:44

I need to explain the structure of this course. Usually the theory is explained right at the start, and you are hit with a pile of terms (saturation, temperature, levels) that are not necessarily understood when they are not put in context.

So what I did was build the course the other way round. First I am going to play with your sensations (*sensaciones*): I will show you how color can change depending on context, and things like that. After that I will start revealing the theory behind it.

In the same way, we will go from something very complex, where many colors are needed to reach the final result, to a very, very simple illustration.

## The editable PSD files

Anchor: 1140 · U1 · L03 · 02:22–03:05

I must confess that one of the things I am proudest of in this course is that I have included PSD files in the tasks. They are my own creations, illustrations I have organized so that you can manipulate them and control the color of an image without having to worry about creating its design, its composition and so on. In the end, this course is mainly about color.

What I want is for everyone who enters this course to be able to feel what happens when we change a color, when we make this or that decision, without being limited by their current drawing level. You will see that this helps you a great deal to gain confidence, so that one day you can create your own images with what you have learned in this course.

### Key points (derived)

- The course teaches a method for planning color from the start of an image and for choosing palettes that fit its feeling and story.
- It runs in reverse: perception and context first, theory afterwards; and from complex, many-color images to very simple illustrations.
- Color decisions start at the setup stage, even in black and white.
- The tasks come with the teacher's editable PSD illustrations, so students can work on color alone without drawing their own images.
- Watch the lessons in order.
