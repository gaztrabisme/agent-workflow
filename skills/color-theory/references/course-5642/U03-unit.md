# Unit 3 · Color applied to graphic design

Anchor: 5642 · U3

Leire Fernández and Eduardo Herrera, PhDs in Fine Arts and experts in graphic design, will teach you the functions and variables of color in this course. Through an analytical and practical approach, you will learn to understand and apply the communicative power of color to improve the impact of your visual projects.

- ### Additional bibliography

These are the bibliographical references of some books that we find interesting to complete this unit:

 *Colorpedia: Color dictionary for designers* \| Adams, S.
  
 Information on color terminology, its cultural significance and history, and examples of specific applications from great artists, architects, designers, etc.

 *The interaction of color* \| Albers, J.
  
 Exhibition of perceptual strategies that help to broaden the way of using and perceiving colors in the visual arts, architecture, textiles, interior design, graphics, etc.

 *Color as a material and visual resource* \| Boerboom, P., and Proetel, T.
  
 Presentation of experiments and exercises to explore the expressive potential of the materiality of color and its effects on our visual perception, based on its visual nature as pigment and visual illusion, matter and cognition.

 *History of colors* \| Brusatin, M.
  
 An exhibition that, starting in the 16th century, goes through Newton, Goethe and reaches Klee, Wittgenstein and the most recent speculations on colour.

 *Designing for the eyes* \| Costa, J.
  
 Reflection on aspects of image perception, graphic semiotics, expressive use of color, information design, graphic languages, as well as the design of everyday life in an experience that is not only visual, but multisensory and multicultural.

 *Theory and use of color* \| De Grandis, L.
  
 Very specific reflections on psychic\-chemical factors, colour analysis, painting technique and practice, visual apparatus, perception and chromatic balance, contrast and assimilation, principles of chromatic differentiation and totalisation, etc.

 *Color in human activities* \| Dériberé, M.
  
 Review of basic color issues: definition, vocabulary, color classification, color vision, physics and physiology of color, psychological effects of color, effects of color on living beings, etc.

 *Color. Project and aesthetics in the graphic arts* \| Fabris and Germani.
  
 A schematic and basic treatise, as an index, of the various problems that may arise with respect to the necessary knowledge of the behavior of color in graphic arts and design.

 *Color harmonies* \| Garau, A.
  
 Using a rigorous method, he approaches the study of the structural links that determine a chromatic composition. He also formulates a theory of color that he exemplifies with the analysis of some works by famous artists such as Cézanne, Picasso and Matisse.

 *Color* \| Gerritse, F.
  
 An overview of color theory from ancient times to the present is provided. Instructions are given for constructing one's own perception model that allows one to see all color values within a chromatic system.

 *Art of the color,* \| Itten, J.
  
 The ideas that Itten developed in the courses he taught at the Bauhaus in Weimar from 1919 to 1923 are presented. A theoretical approach that addresses the essential aspects of colour: construction and harmony, subjective agreements, contrasts, composition, etc.

 *Fundamentals of color theory* \| Küppers, H.
  
 With a didactic profile, the author presents different physical aspects of color, from the physiological aspects to the laws of color mixing, quality characteristics and systematic color tables.

 *Color and Harmony* \| Sawahata, L.
  
 This book offers a guide to possible combinations for different uses of color, from painting a room to painting a picture, with the aim of reflecting harmony, professionalism, calm or passion.

 *Color and Light* \| Tornquist; J.
  
 The author analyses each of the technical foundations of the use of colour based on the current systems for colour classification, as well as the physical and physiological principles on which the phenomenon of perception is based.

 *Introduction to painting through color* \| Truan, M.
  
 A review of the main theoretical themes in the world of colour within its close relationship with the knowledge of Painting. A solid theoretical and practical basis on colour based on analytical and perceptual criteria.

 *The Big Book of Colour* \| Varley, H.
  
 A book that addresses color from multiple perspectives, such as its nature, vision, art, psychology, means of application, fashion, actions, color palette, or chromatic potential.

 *Principles of Color Design,* \| Wong, W.
  
 An introduction to color for both graphic designers and artists. A work that establishes design fundamentals, examining common color principles through different exercises.

 Platform for color inspiration with thousands of proposals for harmonious color ranges.

 Resource to analyze the color ranges of some of the most famous paintings of all time, viewing them by author.

 To create and test color gradient effects from two extreme colors.

 [Material design palett](https://www.materialpalette.com/)
  
 By choosing two colours, combinations of eight harmonious colour ranges are proposed.

 [Adobe Color](https://color.adobe.com/)
  
 Free Adobe tool to test and select color palettes by applying color harmony rules: analogous, monochromatic, triad, complementary, composite, tones, and custom.

 [Paleton](https://paletton.com/)
  
 Creation of harmonious monochromatic, adjacent, triad, tetrad and freestyle color palettes.

 [Canva](https://www.canva.com/colors)
  
 Generator of harmonious color palettes from different resources: color wheel, keywords, from photographs, etc.

 [Cohesive colors](https://javier.xyz/cohesive-colors)
  
 Tool that allows you to establish ranges of specific colors and check the variations in intensity of said colors.

 [TinEye](https://labs.tineye.com/)
  
 Based on a selection of colors (five maximum), this tool allows you to search for images with similar tones in more than ten million photographs with a free Creative Commons license that exist in the vast archives of Flickr.

 [Color Contrast Check](https://snook.ca/technical/colour_contrast/colour)
  
 Tool that allows you to calculate whether the contrast between two colors is sufficient. Difference between normal text and large text (greater than 18 points).

 [Colordot](https://color.hailpixel.com/)
  
 Simply moving the mouse over the screen allows you to select the exact color you are looking for. From left to right to find the exact tone and from top to bottom for luminosity and saturation. Colors can be added to find harmonies and contrasts.

- ### Interesting readings

Here are the bibliographical references of the books that we find interesting to complete this unit.

 *Design and colour: how the language of colour works and how to manipulate it in graphic design* \| Berry, S. and Martin, J.
  
 Nine chapters analyzing opposite pairs of color tones (warm/cool, individual/collective, etc.) from six foundations: tone, saturation, shape, contour, design and texture.

 *Color and Culture: The Practice and Meaning of Color from Antiquity to Abstraction* \| Cage, J.
  
 This book shows how the perception of colour, seemingly immediate and timeless, has a historical dimension. Focusing on the history of art, it links colour to art, philosophy and religious mentality.

 *The theory of colori* \| Goethe, J.W.
  
 With this work, the author laid the foundations for the psychology of color as we know it today, highlighting human perception as the aspect forgotten by the theories on color proposed by science.

 *Color psychology: How colors affect feelings and reason* \| Heller, E.
  
 A study of the relationship of colours to human feelings, demonstrating that their associations are not merely questions of taste, but universal experiences that are deeply rooted in our language and our thinking.

 *Chromaphilia: The story of color in Art* \| Paul, S.
  
 A reflection on the capacity of colour to act as a language or system of signifiers. A resource that inspires emotion and intellect, passion and reason, individuality and collectivity, in equal measure.

 *. The secret lives of color* \| St Clair, K.
  
 A work that contains unusual and fascinating stories about seventy\-five shades of colour. A book that, through colour, helps us to better understand our culture.

 **Russell, D.**
  
 A series of books that allow you to view possible chromatic results for a specific colour. A sample of possibilities in different media to check the behaviour of a colour and the possibilities of combination with other colours.

Here you can consult the bibliography of some books that we find interesting.

 *Corporate signage* \| Costa, J.
  
 The book takes an evolutionary journey through signage, starting from its beginnings as road signs in ancient times, and reaching the current corporate signage that coordinates Identity and Image, Architecture and Design, Information and Technology.

 *Color and und et communication* \| Favre, JP and November, A.
  
 Review of communications theory and analysis of the role of color as a signal and symbol. Color is presented as communication both at the cognitive level (information) and at the effective level (expression).

 *Il linguaggio del colore* \| Luzzatto, L. and Pompas, R.
  
 This book reflects in a clear and simple way on how color is never a random phenomenon, but rather responds to a precise language code.
