# Artist references: what to study in each

Reader: an agent recommending references, or building a mood board, for a target kind of color. Source: [L02](../course/U01-L02-influences.md), with the course's analysed examples from later lessons.

The teacher files color references in three families, arranged as a gradient. (1140 · U1 · L02 · 00:00–00:59)

| Family | Formula | Artists | What to look at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colorful | Many hues at once, still harmonious and natural | Muyang Xu (1140 · U1 · L02 · 00:59–01:41); Floriane Marchix (1140 · U1 · L02 · 01:41–02:25); Nathan Fowkes (1140 · U1 · L02 · 02:25–02:47); Theo Prins (1140 · U1 · L02 · 02:47–03:21) | How bridges and light bounces hold a "rainbow" together. Fowkes is analysed block by block in 1140 · U3 · L10 · 03:19–05:33 |
| Vivid | Few colors, one or two made to read stronger than they are | Jeff Turley (1140 · U1 · L02 · 03:45–04:16); Gop Gap (1140 · U1 · L02 · 04:16–04:47); Joon Ahn (1140 · U1 · L02 · 04:47–05:14); Tobi Trebeljahr (1140 · U1 · L02 · 05:14–05:54); Ryan Lang (1140 · U1 · L02 · 05:54–06:34) | Where the single strong color sits, and what is kept quiet around it |
| Near-black | Almost all dark, color only where it serves the story | Alex Mandradjiev (1140 · U1 · L02 · 06:34–07:47); Nickolai Lockertsen (1140 · U1 · L02 · 07:47–08:14) | How little color is needed for intensity |
| Muted | Grey or desaturated overall, a small fraction of saturated color | Peleng / Sergey Kolesov (1140 · U1 · L02 · 08:14–09:44); Michal Misowski (1140 · U1 · L02 · 09:44–10:40); Joseph Zbukvic (1140 · U1 · L02 · 11:09–11:50); Richard Schmid (1140 · U1 · L02 · 11:50–12:56) | Variety inside low saturation; the one accent |

The near-black group is presented inside the vivid family. It is split out here because it is a distinct recipe.

## Films and examples analysed in the course

- **Minions, color script by Clement Griselain.** Contrast across a sequence. (1140 · U2 · L05 · 06:26–07:51)
- **The Grand Budapest Hotel.** A palette built from local color. (1140 · U2 · L05 · 07:51–09:18)
- **Klaus.** A cyan counterweight (1140 · U3 · L11 · 04:32–06:00), and wooden fences as texture that carries color variety (1140 · U3 · L12 · 06:35–08:08).
- **The Croods.** Warm, cold and warm planes. (1140 · U4 · L17 · 05:51–07:09)
- **Finding Nemo**, analysed with the Movies in Color site. A planned film palette. (1140 · U4 · L14 · 06:47–08:37)
- **Tobi Trebeljahr's monster.** A cold focal point in a warm image. (1140 · U4 · L17 · 04:32–05:51)
- **Gop Gap's butterfly hunters, an ice cream truck, Ross Tran's bird, a shopfront, a child's room.** Story details that spread color. (1140 · U3 · L13 · 06:51–07:48 to 1140 · U3 · L13 · 11:54–13:17)
