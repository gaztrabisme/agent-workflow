# Science crosswalk: where the course method meets color science

Reader: an agent that has applied the course method and needs to know where the method's claims hold, where color science refines them, and which reference to open. The course (Domestika 1140) is the primary system. The vendored `color-expert` skill in [../color-science/](../color-science/SKILL.md) is the secondary source for facts, measurement and code.

Verdicts:
- **Agrees**: science supports the course claim as stated.
- **Refines**: the claim holds in practice, but science adds a correction to apply.
- **Adds**: science covers something the course does not.

## Vocabulary

| Course term | Science term | Verdict | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Value (*valor*): how close to white or black | Lightness (perceptual, relative to context) | Refines | Measure value as OKLCH L or CIELAB L\*, not HSL L or HSB B. HSL gives saturated yellow and saturated blue the same L although they look very different. See [SKILL.md § Understanding HSL's Limitations](../color-science/SKILL.md) and [lightness-vs-brightness.md](../color-science/references/contemporary/lightness-vs-brightness.md). Photoshop's Black & White layer uses adjustable channel weights, so treat it as a check, not a measurement. |
| Saturation (*saturación*): purity, intensity | Chroma (distance from grey at the same lightness) or saturation (colorfulness relative to brightness) | Refines | Course 1140 uses Photoshop HSB S; 5642 also calls mixtures with white, grey, black or a complement "desaturation" (5642 · U3 · L24 · 03:37–05:08). For precise image checks, this method uses **OKLCH chroma**. See [chroma-vs-saturation.md](../color-science/references/contemporary/chroma-vs-saturation.md). |
| Color | Hue | Agrees | |
| Color wheel with complements opposite | Visual complements follow CMY/RGB for screen mixing: red/cyan, yellow/blue, magenta/green | Refines | Course 1140 uses an RGB-based wheel for its screen and afterimage exercises (1140 · U3 · L08 · 14:13–15:39). Course 5642 teaches a traditional RYB design wheel with red/green, yellow/violet and blue/orange pairs (5642 · U3 · L24 · 05:49–06:28). Keep this difference explicit: use RYB when describing that lesson's paint-oriented design convention, and RGB/CMY for screen complement checks. See [ryb-vs-cmy-color-wheels.md](../color-science/references/historical/ryb-vs-cmy-color-wheels.md) and [florent-farges-color-harmony-painters.md](../color-science/references/techniques/florent-farges-color-harmony-painters.md). |
| Functional color roles | Representation, evocation, or a coded sign | Agrees | Course 5642's three functions describe communicative jobs in graphic design, not intrinsic meanings or measurable color properties (5642 · U3 · L26 · 00:55–01:40). Use them to state design intent; validate psychological or symbolic readings with the intended audience. |

## Measuring

The formulas, the Photoshop hue ranges and the default thresholds are in [measurement.md](measurement.md). Two traps to avoid:
- WCAG 2 contrast needs the piecewise sRGB linearization, not APCA's pure 2.4 gamma.
- HSB saturation misreads dark colors, so measure OKLCH chroma instead.
