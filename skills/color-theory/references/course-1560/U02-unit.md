# Unit 2 · Preparation and Theory

Anchor: 1560 · U2

In this unit, I’ll show you my workspace and the tools we’ll use, then I'll introduce you to some very important concepts to understand the logic of light.

## Tasks

- ### Learn to recognize hard and soft light

Go to this unit's attachments and download the folder with images. In each of the provided images, try to recognize if the light is soft or hard, and what is each light source (the sun? the sky? A lamp? A candle? the light bouncing on the walls?).

![](https://assets3.domestika.org/course-images/000/011/713/11713-big.jpg)
- ### Notice how your phone handles exposure

Take your phone camera and observe how the exposure changes automatically when you point it at brighter or darker places. Notice what happens when you change the exposure manually. 

![](https://assets0.domestika.org/course-images/000/011/710/11710-big.jpg)
- ### Learn to recognize reflections

Observe the world around you and try to recognize reflective surfaces. Once you see one, try to see *WHAT* is reflecting on this surface and causing a highlight. Is it a lamp? A window? A screen? You will see it is always a very bright object.

![](https://assets3.domestika.org/course-images/000/011/723/11723-big.jpg)
- ### Create your scenes 
Practice exercise 2\.1

Now it's time to work on your own scenes. It should be a simple character posed in a minimalistic background, I want you to think of it not as a narrative illustration, but rather as a small sandbox to practice in and try out different lightings. Don’t include small details or complex shapes, as they will only slow you down and are not the point of this exercise.

Your character should be made of simple volumes (boxes, cylinders, spheres). Once your drawing is done, you should make a second version with a different background. Share your scenes and give us a brief explanation of why you chose them.

![](https://assets5.domestika.org/course-images/000/011/725/11725-big.jpg)
- ### ¡Paint your local colors!

Make sure your colors aren’t too bright or too dark, you will learn the right balance with time. You can use my file as a reference for how light or dark the colors should be.

![](https://assets4.domestika.org/course-images/000/011/714/11714-big.jpg)
