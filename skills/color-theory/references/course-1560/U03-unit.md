# Unit 3 · Let’s Paint!

Anchor: 1560 · U3

Now that you’ve learned about the important basics of light, it’s time to learn how to apply them to your own paintings. Don’t forget that from now on, your eyedropper needs to be in the “current layer only” mode.

## Tasks

- ### Paint your five lighting situations.

Now it's time to follow my steps for each light situation and paint your own. 

![](https://assets5.domestika.org/course-images/000/011/715/11715-big.jpg)
- ### Paint your own situation 
Practice exercise 3\.1

Paint the scene in a new lighting situation that you come up with, this will allow you to see if you really understand the method. Share with us your new situation and explain why you chose that specific scenario.
