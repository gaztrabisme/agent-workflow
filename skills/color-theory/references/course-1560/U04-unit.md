# Unit 4 · Details, Reflections and Translucency

Anchor: 1560 · U4

Let’s add a few details that will push the realism and believability of our paintings. This unit will serve to demonstrate the principles explained in the lesson of Reflections and Translucency.

## Tasks

- ### Complete all your paintings 
Practice exercise 4\.1

Now it´s time to complete all of your paintings, pay attention to where the light source is when painting a reflection.
  
Remember that less reflection is better than more, otherwise, your character will look like it’s made of plastic! Share your final work here and give us your thoughts on each scene you've created.
