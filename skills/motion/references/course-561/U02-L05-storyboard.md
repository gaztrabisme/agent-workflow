## Planning the story in small frames

Anchor: 561 · U2 · L05 · 00:02–00:45

We are starting to work on the storyboard. We will draw a few small squares to think through the idea of our story and how we will present the information in our infographic. You can do this on paper, in Illustrator, Photoshop, or however you like. In my case, what I am going to tell is very simple, so I do not need to make a storyboard; this is just to give you an idea. You can include animations in the storyboard too: what you are thinking of doing to animate each frame (*cuadro*).

## PayPal information and its modules

Anchor: 561 · U2 · L05 · 01:41–02:33

In the next scene, my idea is to introduce PayPal with a title and something in the subtitle. For the transition from one panel to another, I am thinking of using transparency and position. I keep adding little reminders about transitions and building the story. Since PayPal is about money, the idea is to put small money signs here. Next comes the information box. Since this is still the storyboard and sketches stage, I have not thought it through yet, but we can get an idea of the modules and images we will use. We can introduce circles or whatever we like. These elements can contain profile pictures. This is the information we collected to build the storyboard: facts, images, profile photos, or employees.

## Tesla and SpaceX panels

Anchor: 561 · U2 · L05 · 02:33–03:50

In my case, I want to continue with Tesla. I will have a title and subtitle too, and use the same transition as the other panels: transparency and position. Here again, the idea is to build a grid (*cuadrícula*) with all the information. We can put facts here, bars with information, or sliders (*controles deslizantes*): the small ideas you have about how you are going to tell the story can go into the storyboard. Then we move on to SpaceX. Here, again, we will have information modules. I want to draw a rocket here. It obviously does not need to be very detailed.

## Reviewing the sequence

Anchor: 561 · U2 · L05 · 03:50–04:43

That is everything. It depends a lot on what you want to tell. In my case it is very simple: a title, how it enters with transparency and position, and a transparency-and-position transition to move from one panel to the next. Then we have another title with a subtitle, and we animate everything. We show an information panel, make another transition, introduce the company with its subtitle, and I will probably add some images to illustrate what Tesla is. We show Tesla information and the facts we have collected. Then we move to SpaceX, its subtitles and decorations. Lastly, we show the SpaceX information. That is all. With the storyboard ready, we will make the sketches in Illustrator or Photoshop.
