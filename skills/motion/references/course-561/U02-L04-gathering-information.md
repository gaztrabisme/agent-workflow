## What an infographic is

Anchor: 561 · U2 · L04 · 00:02–00:44

At the start of this unit, we are going to prepare our infographic. I chose to work on Elon Musk, but you can choose whatever you want. I chose to cover his contributions to technology, so we are going to collect information. We tend to take for granted that everyone knows what an infographic is, but perhaps some of you do not. An infographic (*infografía*) is basically a long strip of information containing data and graphics summarized around a specific topic.

## Finding data and visual references

Anchor: 561 · U2 · L04 · 01:25–02:33

The information will always depend on the topic you choose, but finding facts is as simple as going to Wikipedia and pulling out small pieces of information. For PayPal, for example, you can look for the year it was founded, its CEO, its founders, how many employees it has, how much money it has made each year, and many other things. Another thing I sometimes do is go to Google Images and search for “PayPal infographic,” or search for the topic we are working on. This can help me pick small pieces of information from other infographics, or find prompts that give you ideas for other kinds of information to use in your own infographic. You can use any infographic or information about the topic you like best. The important thing is to have facts, as we see on screen. You do not need a lot of information; a couple of facts are enough. They will help us put together and design the scenes later.

## Choosing what to include

Anchor: 561 · U2 · L04 · 02:33–03:28

I already knew a little about Elon and his companies, and I am going to cover three specific topics: PayPal, Tesla, and SpaceX. Simple facts such as active accounts, employees, the founding date, the evolution of his net worth, and some quotes I pulled from Elon will all help us make our animated infographic. In the next lesson, we will start sketching the panels (*placas*), put everything in better order, and discard what is not useful. The idea is to make five to ten panels rather than make the video too long, or it becomes tedious. The goal is a simple design with a distinctive touch in the animation and textures. Now that our information is ready, we will make the storyboard.
