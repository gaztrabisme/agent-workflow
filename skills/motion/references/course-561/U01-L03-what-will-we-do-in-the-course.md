## The course plan

Anchor: 561 · U1 · L03 · 00:00–00:22

What are we going to do? In this course, you will learn how to animate an infographic from scratch. We will see the process of creating an animated project from beginning to end. We will choose a topic and look for references for the infographic.

## Sketching, animating, and exporting

Anchor: 561 · U1 · L03 · 00:22–00:42

With the references, we will make a sketch and then the storyboard. With all of this, we will animate, taking great care with the details of our infographic. As the final piece, we will get a GIF and look at the options for publishing it on social media. In the next unit, we will start working on our project.
