# Unit 2 · Preparation

Anchor: 561 · U2

In this unit we are going to start with the most basic part of the project. We will collect the information for our infographic and make sketches that will allow us to shape the story we are going to tell.

## Tasks

- ### Collect information 
Practice exercise 2\.1

Collect information on what you like the most, the cosmos, the weather, cars, sports, whatever you want. The main idea is that you gather some information to make a couple of plates and then animate them. If you can't think of anything special, you can search for infographics on Pinterest. Take them to Illustrator or Photoshop and give it your style. It doesn't matter if you don't feel comfortable designing. You can copy the icons and designs, the idea is that you keep practicing and that you can have all the digital material separated into layers so that you can later animate it. 

![](https://assets4.domestika.org/course-images/000/011/414/11414-big.png)
- ### Sketches 
Practice exercise 2\.2

Here you are free to make the sketches wherever you want, on paper, in Photoshop, Illustrator, on a tablet or a cell phone. The important thing is that you remove the rust from that doll and start throwing ideas and sketches, without fear, any idea no matter how small or crazy it seems can become a great animation.
