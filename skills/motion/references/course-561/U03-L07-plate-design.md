## Planning the plates

Anchor: 561 · U3 · L07 · 00:02–01:42

In this unit, we will get fully into designing the plates (*placas*). At this stage we will design three plates. My infographic is divided into four parts: first, the main title, with a little information about the topics I will cover; then each subtopic—PayPal, Tesla, and SpaceX—with its presentation plate and an information plate. That is all. Depending on the topic you chose, you will need more or fewer scenes to design. The idea is to make five or six plates; there is no need to make so many, because the goal is to keep it simple and give each element detail. On the first plate, since it is an introduction, I added a little of everything. I simply used words, symbols, and a few icons.

## Set a consistent visual system

Anchor: 561 · U3 · L07 · 01:43–02:44

Always try to keep the style consistent. Do not use too many colors. Use the same typeface, if possible, throughout the design—one or two, at most three. If you can, use specific sizes for each thing. For example, make titles one size and bold; set important information or highlighted numbers in bold; and, if you use lines, keep their strokes the same size. This will make everything neater and more orderly. You will establish a system, and everything will feel more in place.
