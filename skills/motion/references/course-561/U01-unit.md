- ### Michael Rigley and Ash Thorp

[Michael Rigley](http://www.michaelrigley.com/) and [Ash Thorp](https://altcinc.com/) did together a project of animated titles for FITC \- TOKYO. This job was a before and after in my life. It was when I said, and I want to animate and design like that, with that level. Michael is more inclined to design and 3D animation creating incredible projects and worlds. He has worked for Google, Intel, Adobe, Audi, and Nissan among others. Ash Thorp works in both 2D and 3D. He has done projects for Call of Duty, Ghost in the Shell, Nike, and Assassins Creed among others.

I invite you to visit their profiles, you will not regret it, they have very good projects, from which we can be inspired and learn a lot.
