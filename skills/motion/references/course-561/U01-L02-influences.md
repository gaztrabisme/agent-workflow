## Jorge Canedo and his animation work

Anchor: 561 · U1 · L02 · 00:00–01:07

Now I’m going to show you some of my main influences. As an animator, you often think that giving each animation a little time is enough. That’s what I thought until I came across Jorge Canedo’s work, which you can see here. When you pay attention to the smallest animation detail, that’s when you make a difference. It doesn’t matter whether the animations are simple or complex. I learned all this from Jorge’s work: the stories are often simple, told with basic shapes, but animated at an incredible level. Like many of us, Jorge learned animation on his own. He created the Wine After Coffee channel, where they share animations at a very high level. They are very good, serve as inspiration, and offer a lot to learn from. He is also the organizer of the Blend Festival, held in Canada, which brings together the best animators for talks every year.

## Michael Rigley and Ash Thorp’s work

Anchor: 561 · U1 · L02 · 01:31–02:21

Michael leans more toward 3D design and creates incredible worlds. He has worked for Intel and Google, and on games such as Destiny, with car brands such as Audi or Nissan. Ash Thorp also designs in 3D, but he leans more toward 2D. He has worked on Assassin’s Creed and made many very good game openings, such as Call of Duty. I invite you to look at their work; you won’t regret it. He has very good projects that we can draw inspiration from and learn a lot from. Now that we’ve talked about my influences, let’s see what we’ll do in the course.
