# Unit 3 · Design and preparation

Anchor: 561 · U3

Well now it's time for us to get serious ... or not, who cares! We already have the sketches, now we are going to refine those lines. We will align the material and give color and life to our designs. First we will design the plates and then we will take the designs to Photoshop to finally texture our images.

## Tasks

- ### Lights and shadows 
Practice exercise 3\.1

In many cases the texturing includes highlights and shadows. That is why I order you to make a small study of the lights and shadows based on their designs. For example [here](https://ar.pinterest.com/pin/815151601292041507/) can see how different geometric objects receive light and cast shadows. Use the elements you have in your design and imagine a light source and how it would look on your object and where the shadow would be projected. 

![](https://assets6.domestika.org/course-images/000/011/416/11416-big.png)
- ### Textures 
Practice exercise 3\.2

Following the videos now I challenge you to apply texture to your designs. As I said in the class, it is not necessary to apply texture to every object in your design. You can apply a general texture to the background and another one on top of everything. You can add only noise or if you are animated you can give textures, lights and shadows to various objects of your design. As I said, it is not necessary to go crazy, the idea is to learn little by little, but if you want to go crazy, always give a little more detail to our design.
