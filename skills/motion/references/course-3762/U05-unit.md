# Unit 5 · Animating the Artwork: From Static to Dynamic

Anchor: 3762 · U5

It's time for our cocktails to start moving! In this unit we will begin to animate. We will start with some fundamental principles and then we will immediately start putting our elements in the timeline. We will work the sequences and transitions to make them dynamic. In the end, we will teach you how to export your work.

## Additional resources

- ### Principles of animation

["The illusion of life"](https://vimeo.com/93206523)

 This video describes the principles of animation formulated by Frank Thomas and Ollie Johnston. We can enrich our animations by applying these principles through Adobe After Effects parameters.
- ### After Effects Plugins

**Velocity curves**

 Within the course the curves and influence parameters have been treated manually, but there are plugins that allow the automation of the curves of a movement.

 [Flow](https://aescripts.com/flow/)

 [Dojo Ease](https://creativedojo.net/store/dojo-ease-v1/)

 **Color samples**

 [Ray Dynamic Color 2](https://aescripts.com/ray-dynamic-color/)

 It is not possible to manage color swatches in After Effects like in Illustrator. However through this plugin it is possible to implement this function for color management.
- ### Climate data initiative

[Meltdown Flags](https://www.meltdownflags.org/)

 Multidisciplinary project that combines digital and analogue, infographics and animation, narrating the melting of glaciers and climate change through the alteration of the flags of individual nations.
