## From the sketch to the sequence

Anchor: 3762 · U3 · L09 · 00:00–00:32

We have seen how to work with drawing. From here, we will explore the possibilities of animation through a storyboard. Let's begin! You saw with Riccardo how to set up the sketch (*schizzo*). Now we will think about the sequence. Imagine a sequence in which all the cocktails are finished.

## Using visual metaphors to plan movement

Anchor: 3762 · U3 · L09 · 00:32–00:48

It is essential to understand what the movements will be and what we intend to tell. In our case, we will use visual metaphors: floating and passing from one form to another. The data, represented by shapes, will float in space, as though they were immersed in a medium.

## A shared element carries the transition

Anchor: 3762 · U3 · L09 · 00:48–01:22

Let's think about how to move from one cocktail to the next. Imagine a concept similar to a recipe: the ingredients will vary from cocktail to cocktail, but we will always keep one element in common. This element will be the center of the animation and guide the transition between the different drinks, so that each component adapts harmoniously to the atmosphere. In this way, the ingredients of the first cocktail will transform into those of the second, creating a sequence.
