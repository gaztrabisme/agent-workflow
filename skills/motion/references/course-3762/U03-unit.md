# Unit 3 · From Inspiration to Storyboard

Anchor: 3762 · U3

Let's start our project: create an animated infographic about the world of cocktails! First, we will begin to do some visual research and create a moodboard with the images that impressed us. Then we will move on to simplifying the shapes with a first sketch before imagining the shapes in motion through the storyboard.

## Tasks

- ### Do some visual research and create your moodboard

Start looking online for images that strike you. Also look at books and magazines. Be guided by instinct! Remember that inspiration can come from the most diverse fields. Then create your moodboard with the images you have chosen.

![](https://assets0.domestika.org/course-images/000/040/500/40500-big.png)
- ### Start working on the sketch 
Practice exercise 3\.1

Now that your visual references are ready, leave room for your creativity and start imagining the shapes and spaces of your project. You can sketch on paper, like I did, or use Photoshop or Illustrator.

![](https://assets8.domestika.org/course-images/000/040/808/40808-big.jpg)
- ### Build your project storyboard 
Practice exercise 3\.2

Once the shapes and spaces are clear, you can focus on how they will move in the scene.



![](https://assets9.domestika.org/course-images/000/040/809/40809-big.jpg)
