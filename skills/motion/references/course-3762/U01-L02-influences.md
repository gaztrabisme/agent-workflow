## Max Bill in industrial design

Anchor: 3762 · U1 · L02 · 01:26–02:18

What seem to us to be simple colored shapes are actually Kelly's way of shaping reality and what surrounds him. This has inspired me to take care with details and to pay attention to my surroundings.

Another aspect of his work that fascinates me is his study of small, everyday details, whether in buildings or natural elements. Many of his paintings draw on shadows or fleeting moments that most people overlook: moments when a particular light falls on an object.

What you see here is one of his paintings, inspired by the shadows in this photograph.

We can notice the same thing in this work: a notebook collecting some sketches. The drawings were inspired by a bus trip, capturing fleeting moments and everyday details, as well as the shadows cast on the book he was reading. This influenced all his work and all his black-and-white works on shadows for many years.

## Emotional response and chance

Anchor: 3762 · U1 · L02 · 04:00–04:35

Ellsworth Kelly is very important to me because he also taught me that the emotional quality (*emozionalità*) of a work is essential. In this series of paintings, we see simple geometric shapes forming a pattern. Each of us responds to them with a different emotion.

To do this, Ellsworth Kelly (the captions render his name here as *elson Orkelly*) arranged the colors randomly in this series. The artist does not control the work; the emotional quality emerges and takes over, as if it could come out and play its part.

## GMUNK: experimentation across media

Anchor: 3762 · U1 · L02 · 04:35–06:16

Like Riccardo, I also want to talk about the inspirations and designers who led me to this work. The first artist I want to tell you about is GMUNK, a designer, director, and digital artist. For me, he has been a point of reference in visual design and research.

His works cover different fields, and their visual experimentation is palpable. His style is marked by attention to detail and evocative atmospheres. The main themes are identity, the subconscious, and the relationship between humans and machines. His experimental approach uses a range of different media and technologies. He is a designer who has explored the potential of augmented reality (*realtà aumentata*), and he has also set standards in cinematic user interfaces (*UI cinematografiche*) and inspired generations of artists. What inspires me most is his ability to move across different fields while remaining recognisable despite the different media and languages.

## Emanuele Colombo: movement carries a message

Anchor: 3762 · U1 · L02 · 06:16–07:27

Another reference I want to talk about is Emanuele Colombo, an animator and motion designer. Thanks to him, I realised the potential of animation for conveying a message. He is not an illustrator, but by using simple shapes, their interaction, and movement, he can tell deep and complex stories.
