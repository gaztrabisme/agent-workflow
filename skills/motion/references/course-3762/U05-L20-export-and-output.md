## Sending the video to Media Encoder

Anchor: 3762 · U5 · L20 · 00:24–01:26

When the animation is finished, export the video through Adobe Media Encoder. Choose File > Export > Add to Media Encoder Queue. The first item opens the export settings, where you can review the output format, duration, encoding details and estimated file size. For the final project, use the H.264 codec.

Click Main next to the output name to choose the file name and destination. Confirm the name and destination, click OK, and press Play to start rendering.

## Adapting the artwork across formats

Anchor: 3762 · U5 · L20 · 02:51–03:24

For this animation, separate compositions (*composizioni*) let us adapt the work to different supports and formats. This lets us isolate individual elements or show specific information on different devices and platforms. Good planning makes it easy to move between digital and print; the boundary is subtle, and you decide what your final project can become.

Explore different themes and play with shapes, identifying elements that characterize your project. Enjoy exploring the theme from several points of view. In the next video, we will give you advice on the final project.
