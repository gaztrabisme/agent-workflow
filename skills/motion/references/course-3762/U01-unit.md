- ### Our inspirations

We have collected sites and books for you to learn more about the work of the artists that we have presented to you in this unit.

 **Ellsworth Kelly**
  
 [Ellsworth Kelly Foundation](https://ellsworthkelly.org/foundation/)
  
 Site that collects the life and works of the American artist.

 [*Ellsworth Kelly* \| Tricia Y. Paik](https://www.phaidon.com/store/art/ellsworth-kelly-9780714876429/)
  
 This book published by Phaidon collects the artist's works and interviews.

 **Max Bill**
  
 [*Max Bill, painter, sculptor, architect, designer . Exhibition catalog* \| T. Buchsteiner and Otto Letze](https://www.ibs.it/max-bill-pittore-scultore-architetto-libro-vari/e/9788837044565#cc-anchor-dettagli)
  
 Here you can find the catalog of Max Bill's 2006 exhibition which contains many works and interviews.

 **Gmunk**
  
 [Website](https://gmunk.com/)
  
 Designer, director and digital artist. On his website you can observe how he approaches his projects through heterogeneous means in a perspective of visual research and experimentation.

 **Emanuele Colombo**
  
 Here you will find the works of Emanuele Colombo, where complex objects are synthesized through geometric shapes.

 [Website](http://www.emanuelecolombo.it/)
  
 [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ema_colombo/)
