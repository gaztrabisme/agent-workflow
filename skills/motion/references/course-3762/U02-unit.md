- ### Collect data

Recognizing, understanding and choosing data is very important. Here are three sites where you can get information about the data.

 [Data Viz Project](https://datavizproject.com/)
  
 Site that contains many examples of data visualization and explains when and how to use them.

 [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/)
  
 Large online archive of data collected from trusted sources and distributed for free.

 [International Bartenders Association](https://iba-world.com/)
  
 Official site of the International Bartenders Association, from which we have taken the official data of the cocktails for our project.

[Design Is History](http://www.designishistory.com/)
