# Unit 4 · Build a story

Anchor: 433 · U4

In this unit we will begin to build our history, for which we will know some tools that will be of great help to you, if you manage to take advantage of them.

## Tasks

- ### Build your own story 
Practice exercise 4\.1

Make a basic storyboard of your interpretation of the Voice Over that I have given you. Do not forget to apply all the tools exposed in this unit (characters, transitions and visual analogies).
