# Unit 3 · X-ray of a story

Anchor: 433 · U3

When we are spectators of a story we only see its outside, but from behind we can discover that it is built on a skeleton that gives it shape. In this unit we will take an x\-ray of the history, in order to understand its components.

## Tasks

- ### Voice Over works

Read the Voice Over I gave you earlier and analyze it. See your possibilities and write concepts that come to mind, loose ideas ... start doing yours.
- ### Premise, tone and structure 
Practice exercise 3\.1

Present your own premise, tone and structure, based on the Voice Over that I have given you. Write it as simply and clearly as possible. Remember to keep the balance between specific enough to understand what we are talking about and general enough so that we do not close narrative or visual possibilities.
