# Unit 5 · Bring a story to life

Anchor: 433 · U5

In this unit we will fully enter the animatic, and we will analyze the use of audio as a narrative tool.

## Tasks

- ### Build your own storyboard 
Practice exercise 5\.1

To be able to work in the animatic, you can use the basic storyboard with which I put my interpretation of the story, there is no problem, but you also have the option to re\-draw it in a more finished way, or to ask a friend or colleague who draw well. The important thing is to preserve and respect the essence of your original story.
- ### Work the audio

Search audios (.wav) on the internet to musicalize and / or sound your story. Remember: it does not matter so much the sound quality of the audio, but rather the information that they give to your animatic.
