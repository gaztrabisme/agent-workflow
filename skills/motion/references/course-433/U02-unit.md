# Unit 2 · Storytelling, the basis for a good story

Anchor: 433 · U2

In this unit we will analyze the reason why every Motion Design project needs a good storytelling, and what you should take into consideration before starting to work on it.

## Tasks

- ### Analysis

Review the three videos of the Amparo \& Justicia campaign and analyze them in detail. Look for the logic that led to making the decisions that shape the stories. Pay attention to the transitions, visual analogies and look for those details that make the three videos are related to each other.
- ### Plan

Based on the three pillars that I present, plan in general terms what your work process will be for this course.
- ### Trip

Download and print the text The Journey. We will work on it in the next unit.
