## Dribbble and Tumblr

Anchor: 503 · U6 · L23 · 01:08–02:05

503 · U06 · L23 · 01:08–02:05

Let's start with Dribbble, a social network built around what they call Shots: these small pieces. Let's go here to see more work. It's very design-focused, with a lot of cool animation. You can sign up, but you have to be drafted (*draftear*) to get all the options for promoting your work. It has a slightly elitist element, but it's fun, and you can make micro-animations (*microanimaciones*) very quickly so they get noticed. In the end, it's a social network for building relationships and promoting your work.

Now we're at my old friend Tumblr, a classic I use to upload my stranger things, my nerdy stuff and the things I quickly make up. I really like it. Since it's a bit like the back yard of the design world, you can let yourself do more crazy things and have a less polished, more punky look. It may have the least visibility for promoting your work, but Tumblr is a lot of fun.

And then we get to Instagram, which I think is currently the jewel in the crown and the platform our course has focused on. The advantage is that everyone is here right now. ## Choose relevant hashtags

Anchor: 503 · U6 · L23 · 03:32–04:36

503 · U06 · L23 · 03:32–04:36

My last piece of advice is to search for the most popular hashtags at the moment you publish. What I'm showing here is a quick search I made; the data is from January 2019. At the moment, the most popular hashtag for motion graphics seems to be #motiongraphics, with 20% popularity. Next is #animation, but the problem with that hashtag is that it's more general. Then #motiondesign comes quite a bit after #motiongraphics, so for now #motiongraphics is winning. #aftereffects is also always an interesting hashtag to use to promote your work.

I recommend that you don't add a huge number of hashtags unrelated to what you're making, because people don't like that and it's deceptive. Use only hashtags that relate to and are relevant to what you're posting. Search and use the ones people use most or the ones that are trending at the time.
