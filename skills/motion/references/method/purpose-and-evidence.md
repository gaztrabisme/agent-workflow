# Purpose and evidence: decide what the motion must do

## Reader and scope

**Reader:** a planner beginning a motion piece. **Scope:** establish the communication purpose and factual substrate before choosing a visual or movement treatment. For a static chart decision, hand off to `information-design`.

## First idea, stated as the rule

- Plan the whole project: identify who speaks, who receives the message, how long it should run, how much production time exists, the response sought from the viewer and the maker's own objective. (433 · U2 · L04 · 00:00–01:03; 433 · U2 · L04 · 01:03–01:56; 433 · U2 · L04 · 01:56–02:46; 433 · U2 · L04 · 02:46–04:12)
- Start from the input, such as a brief, script or voice-over, then decide how it will be told. (433 · U2 · L04 · 04:12–04:42; 433 · U3 · L05 · 00:50–01:41)
- Check data and source links before using them; the course says they must be accurate and carefully validated. (3762 · U2 · L03 · 04:58–05:50)
- A small selection of facts can support a short information sequence; the animated-infographics teacher suggests roughly five to ten panels for the course project. Treat that as this course's guidance, not a required length for other work. (561 · U2 · L04 · 01:25–02:33; 561 · U2 · L04 · 02:33–03:28)

## A rule you derived (derived)

Make a compact evidence ledger before design: claim or value, source record, unit/date if relevant, and whether it is verified or still open. This combines the course's source-check instruction with its separation of supplied input from the way it is told; neither lesson prescribes this ledger. (derived from 3762 · U2 · L03 · 04:58–05:50; 433 · U2 · L04 · 04:12–04:42)

## Failures

**Problem 1: the production objective is missing.**
- **The failure:** the story's target and available production time remain undefined, so the planner cannot judge whether its scope is workable. (433 · U2 · L04 · 01:56–02:46; 433 · U2 · L04 · 02:46–04:12)
- **The fix:** record receiver, intended response, runtime and available production time before setting a story or motion target.

**Problem 2: an information claim has no source.**
- **The failure:** the rendered work can look plausible while its data or source link is wrong. (3762 · U2 · L03 · 04:58–05:50)
- **The fix:** trace the claim to its source, preserve its unit and date, and remove or mark it open if the record cannot support it. (derived from 3762 · U2 · L03 · 04:58–05:50)
