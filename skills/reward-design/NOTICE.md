# Source notice

This skill distills cited research papers, public documentation, public industry accounts and bibliographic material. Finding IDs resolve through `references/evidence-map.md` to the supplied audit records; independent corrections in `audit/research/V/claims-verified.md` govern the synthesis.

No copyrighted text is reproduced beyond short quotes. The references use original summaries and paraphrases; they do not reproduce books, articles or paid course material. Source titles, author credits and citation metadata identify the original works. Copyright in those works remains with their respective holders.

The book list is based on checked descriptions and edition records, not full readings of every book. Design translations and ethical replacements are identified as inferences or recommendations rather than source-tested audience effects.

`scripts/check_refs.py` is copied unchanged from this repository's skill-builder package to provide local package checks. The existing audit packet is retained as supplied, including its original findings and access limitations; corrected tiers are carried in the new skill and evidence map.
