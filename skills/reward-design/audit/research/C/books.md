# Books encountered — Unit C

Research date: 10 October 2026. This is a discovery list, not a claim that the books were read in full or independently validated. “Science” includes scholarly theory/synthesis and does not by itself assign a T1 evidence tier. Film criticism is grouped with practitioner craft. Dates refer to the identified edition; translations and later editions are distinguished where needed. Links were located in this session.

| Title | Author/editor | Year | What it offers | Classification / source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Sweet Anticipation: Music and the Psychology of Expectation* | David Huron | 2006; paperback 2008 | ITPRA, learned expectation, tension, cadence, surprise, and musical emotion; the main planning framework for C1. | Science/theory — [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262582780/sweet-anticipation/) |
| *Voice Leading: The Science behind a Musical Art* | David Huron | 2016 | Auditory perception and how simultaneous musical parts remain understandable; useful for arranging layers around narration. | Science — [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262537384/voice-leading/) |
| *Audio-Vision: Sound on Screen* | Michel Chion; edited/translated by Claudia Gorbman | 1994 English edition; second edition 2019 | Sound–picture relations, synchresis, and how sound changes the interpretation of images. | Practitioner/film theory — [Columbia University Press](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/audio-vision-sound-on-screen/9780231185882/) |
| *The Voice in Cinema* | Michel Chion; translated by Claudia Gorbman | 1999 English edition | The dramatic power of visible and unseen voices, and the relation between a voice and its apparent source. | Practitioner/film theory — [Columbia University Press](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/the-voice-in-cinema/9780231108232/) |
| *Film, a Sound Art* | Michel Chion; translated by Claudia Gorbman | 2009 English edition | Historical and analytical examples of audiovisual language, sound technology, and directorial style. | Practitioner/film theory — [Columbia University Press](https://cup.columbia.edu/book/film-a-sound-art/9780231137768/) |
| *David Lynch* | Michel Chion | 1995 first English edition; revised 2006 edition encountered | Case-based analysis of a filmmaker whose sound and image can create ambiguity and tension; not controlled psychological evidence. | Practitioner/film criticism — [edition information](https://books.google.com/books/about/David_Lynch.html?id=ZzRcAAAAMAAJ) |
| *The Films of Jacques Tati* | Michel Chion | 1997 English edition | Film examples relevant to comic timing and expressive, sometimes nonliteral sound; a discovery lead, not read for this unit. | Practitioner/film criticism — [library record](https://www.finna.fi/Record/kavi_kirjasto.7504) |
| *Designing Sound* | Andy Farnell | 2010 | Procedural construction and analysis of sound effects, using accessible software; helps implement a chosen auditory concept. | Practitioner, with acoustics/DSP — [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262014410/designing-sound/) |
| *Sound Design: The Expressive Power of Music, Voice, and Sound Effects in Cinema* | David Sonnenschein | 2001 | A sound-map workflow connecting story, voice, music, effects, and production choices. | Practitioner — [contents and publication information](https://www.filmsound.org/bibliography/sounddesignbook.htm) |
| *Super Sonic Logos: The Power of Audio Branding* | David Allan | 2021 | Audio-branding cases including Intel, NBC, THX, Windows, and McDonald's; useful industry craft and interview leads. | Practitioner/case studies — [book information](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/super-sonic-logos/9781637420836/text/backmatter04.xhtml) |
| *Emotion and Meaning in Music* | Leonard B. Meyer | 1956 | Foundational account of musical expectation and emotion; historical context for Huron rather than current neurochemical proof. | Science/theory — [publication record](https://books.google.com/books?id=QXniAAAAMAAJ) |
| *Music and Emotion: Theory and Research* | Patrik N. Juslin and John A. Sloboda, editors | 2001 | Research chapters spanning perception, performance, psychophysiology, and everyday listening contexts. | Science — [Oxford University Press](https://academic.oup.com/book/53643) |
| *Affective Neuroscience: The Foundations of Human and Animal Emotions* | Jaak Panksepp | 1998 | Broad biological accounts of emotional systems; background on emotional responses, not an editing formula or a license to equate dopamine with pleasure. | Science — [Oxford University Press](https://academic.oup.com/book/53534) |
| *The Cambridge Companion to Video Game Music* | Melanie Fritsch and Tim Summers, editors | 2021 | Game-music creation/programming, analysis, perception, psychology, and cultural context. | Science/scholarly collection with practitioner material — [Cambridge University Press](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/cambridge-companion-to-video-game-music/DF1838B7C7C5251D7FD77BCC1F862FEB) |
| *The Mozart Effect for Children: Awakening Your Child's Mind, Health, and Creativity with Music* | Don G. Campbell | 2000 | Popular family-oriented music claims; included as a myth-history lead, not evidence that Mozart improves intelligence. | Practitioner/popular advice, scientifically unvalidated for the IQ claim — [WorldCat](https://search.worldcat.org/title/The-Mozart-effect-for-children-%3A-awakening-your-child%27s-mind-health-and-creativity-with-music/oclc/1036775513) |
| *Auditory Interfaces: The Use of Non-Speech Audio at the Interface* | William Buxton, William Gaver, and Sara Bly | 1994, unpublished manuscript | Historical interface-audio concepts and a bibliography; encountered through a research report citation, not used as evidence. | Practitioner/research manuscript, not a published book — [citation encountered](https://projekter.aau.dk/projekter/files/14427084/CH_Report_without_abstract.pdf) |
| *The Art of Public Speaking*, 13th edition | Stephen E. Lucas | 2019 | Broad speaking and rehearsal craft; a secondary lead for delivery work, not controlled evidence for pitch/pace prescriptions. | Practitioner — [bibliographic citation encountered](https://portal.findresearcher.sdu.dk/files/277337725/EmbodiedVoices24-NonverbalCommSignalsSummary_CAMERA_READY.pdf) |
