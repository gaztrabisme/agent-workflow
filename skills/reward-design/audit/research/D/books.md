# Books encountered

This is a discovery inventory, not a claim that the books were read in full or that their advice is scientifically validated. It includes relevant books encountered in source texts, author catalogs and bibliographic searches. Years identify the first edition unless another edition is specified. “Science” includes popular research synthesis, which still requires checking individual claims; “practitioner” indicates craft, business guidance or professional case analysis. The sources below distinguish accessible material from catalog-only discovery.
