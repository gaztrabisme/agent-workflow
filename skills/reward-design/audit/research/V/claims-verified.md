# Independent claim check — reward-design

Run: 2026-10-10-reward-skill. Checked all **129 IDs**: **122 supported, 7 weakened, 0 unsupported**. A–D: 100 rows (93 supported, 7 weakened); E: 29 books, all bibliographically supported. By unit: A 23/2/0, B 24/1/0, C 22/3/0, D 24/1/0, E 29/0/0 (supported/weakened/unsupported).

The five most important corrections:

1. **Use the specified tier categories consistently.** A10's pharmacological behavior/skin-conductance experiment is T3, with 27 analyzed after 29 completed. A18's EEG and C4's DTI are T1 because they measure the brain, despite single-study uncertainty. C1's scholarly expectation theory is T3. T1 is a method category, not proof of replication, dopamine, causation or useful media transfer. [Drug-study methods](https://www.ub.edu/brainvitge/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Ferreri_PNAS_2019-1.pdf), [EEG study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32194279/), [DTI study](https://mindlab.research.wesleyan.edu/files/2014/01/Sachs-Ellis-Schlaug-Loui-2016-scan-chills-dti.pdf).
2. **B12: qualify prediction-before-reveal benefits.** The 2017 result is real, but a 2018 follow-up found task-dependent benefits and no clear true-distribution recall advantage. Use T3 for the cited prediction recipe and avoid a generally replicated recall promise. [Primary follow-up](https://users.eecs.northwestern.edu/~jhullman/imagining_replications.pdf).
3. **C20: downgrade musical branding to proof of concept, T3.** Four related studies do not establish independently replicated effectiveness of the composition method; selection, training, small samples and forced-choice matching constrain transfer. [Primary branding article](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/270626731_Developing_a_functional_method_to_apply_music_in_branding_Design_language-generated_music).
4. **D12: correct the attribution evidence.** Pixar's lesson exists, but the alleged Kenn Adams credit in that specific video could not be independently retrieved. Cite Adams's own 2013 account for his 1991 invention; do not represent the inaccessible video credit as verified. [Creator’s account](https://www.aerogrammestudio.com/2013/06/05/back-to-the-story-spine/).
5. **E: remove blanket empirical tiers from books.** Existence, academic authorship and a neuroscience subject do not make a narrative book T1 or T2. Grade research claims individually; treat practitioner frameworks as T4. Retain edition/release/copyright distinctions and contributor credits noted below.
