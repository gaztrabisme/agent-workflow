# Books encountered — Unit B

These 45 relevant books surfaced in the retained papers, their reference trails, or focused publisher/author searches. This is a bibliographic resource list, not a full-book review. “Science” means a research synthesis, textbook or historical scientific work; “practitioner” means design guidance, craft or an interpretive argument. Neither label establishes a dopamine effect. Years identify the listed edition; older interface examples need updating for current devices and audiences.

The Routledge search result for the multiple-representations handbook has an unrelated-looking URL slug but displays the correct title, editors, ISBN and 2020 copyright.
