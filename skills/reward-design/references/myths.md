## Application boundaries

Reject the unsupported universal rule without rejecting every underlying effect. The red/blue failure concerns the specified anagram task; the priming caution concerns the cited walking task; Mozart evidence permits some temporary spatial-task differences; peak/end evidence still supports qualified contributions. These are not findings that colour, priming, music or endings never matter. (T2; B11) (T3; E22) (T1; C24, A25)

Do not substitute another fixed number after debunking one. Platform metric checkpoints, experimental exposure settings, musical phrases and prototype timings serve different purposes. Label which kind of number is used. (T3; A21, A22, B25) (T4; D1, D4, D5)
