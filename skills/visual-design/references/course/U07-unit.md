# Unit 7 · How to think like a graphic designer

Anchor: 5642 · U7

- ### Recommendations for making a presentation for screen

**Page layout**

 • Question the format.

 • One screen, one concept.

 • Structure the page with a single axis and be aware of it throughout the document.

 • Start designing the presentation with the most complex page.

 **Composition**

 • Do not design empty text columns.

 • Do not distribute empty spaces.

 • Follow the reading direction to narrate visually: from left to right.

 **Typography**

 • Don’t use “thematic” lyrics.

 • Work with a typeface family.

 • Do not highlight words by writing them in capital letters, respect spelling.

 • Do not separate words in titles.

 **Images**

 • Do not put logos inside the text.

 • Avoid colors that vibrate or do not contrast enough.

 • Don't use meaningless images to liven up the page and make sure they have a consistent graphic style.
- ### Tips for making a business card

**General design**

 • Consider the format. Standards are sometimes useful – they exist for a reason.

 • Remember that leaves have a back, that can be useful.

 • Good design is a statement, sometimes it's something you can easily describe in words.

 **Images**

 • Use meaningful images, not to evoke something we won't find

 • Use colors to help differentiate between figure and background. Avoid the “window” effect on the page.

 **Typography**

 • Make sure some typographic characters are correct.

 • Avoid using certain fonts in uppercase, especially script.

 • Instead of text that looks hand\-drawn, do something by hand.

 • Develop a stylistically coherent logo.

 • Choose fonts that look good at the size they are used in.

 **Composition**

 • Do not distribute elements across the page.

 • If you don't know where to place loose elements, look for the internal structure of the piece to support them.

 **Colour**

 • In design, colors rarely have meaning, but almost always connote.

 • Take advantage of the power of spot colors.
- ### Recommendations for creating an identity for a publishing house

**Typography**

 • The justified block is designed for bodies of text, do not overuse it in logos.

 • You can combine different weights to compensate for different font sizes.

 • Avoid loose particles at the end of the line.

 • Avoid false small caps.

 • Do not combine two similar fonts.

 **Iconography**

 • Avoid obvious and easily forgotten images.

 • Create an logo with the most important application in mind.

 • Use a graphical system that allows you to grow indefinitely and not according to the applications.
