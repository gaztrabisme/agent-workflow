# Unit 6 · Graphic design from simple geometries

Anchor: 5642 · U6

Learn to create graphic resources and visual identity systems from simple geometric shapes such as circles, squares and triangles.

 In this module, Esteban Ibarra will teach you how to design striking visual identities by exploring the communicative power of geometry. Upon completion, you will be able to develop graphic resources for brands and tell visual stories with basic shapes.

- ### Recommended readings

If you want to delve even deeper into the world of design, art and visual studies, I recommend these books that are essential for working as a designer.

 [*Shapes. Geometric Forms in Graphic Design* \| Shaoqiang Wang](https://www.amazon.com/Shapes-Geometric-Graphic-Design-Elements/dp/8416504547)
  
 Interesting book that addresses the use of geometry in graphic design.

 [*Signs, symbols, brands, signals* \| Adrian Frutiger](https://editorialgg.com/signos-simbolos-marcas-se-ales-libro.html)
  
 Much of this book by Adrian Frutiger—essential for any design professional—covers sign analysis with the aim of finding the presence of simple formal constants that, in successive communications, form the basic signs (triangle, cross, arrow, etc.).
- ### Gestalt laws

["Exploring the Gestalt Principles of Design"](https://www.toptal.com/designers/ui/exploring-the-gestalt-principles-of-design)
  
 In this link, you will find clear examples of some of the laws that our eyes and brain put into play when they come into contact with shapes arranged on a plane.
- ### Grills

[*Grid Systems in Graphic Design. A Visual Communication Manual for Graphic Designers, Typographers and Three Dimensional Designers* \| Josef Müller\-Brockmann](https://www.niggli.ch/Produkt/grid-systems-in-graphic-design/)
  
 This author helped spread knowledge about grids around the world. This book is almost foundational for a designer. It provides guidelines and rules for the function and use of grid systems from 8 to 32 grid fields that can be used in a variety of applications. use for the most varied projects.
- ### The elements

["The First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid with Colored Diagrams and Symbols"](https://www.c82.net/euclid/)
  
 *The Elements* is a mathematical and geometric treatise consisting of thirteen books, written by the Greek mathematician Euclid around 300 BC in Alexandria. The C82 portal developed a reproduction of Oliver Byrne's famous work from 1847 plus interactive diagrams, cross\-references and posters designed by Nicholas Rougeux. Very interesting!

 The pictographic work of this amazing German designer is a great example of universal thinking based on geometry. I'm sure it can be of great help to you for this project and for the future.

- ### Mock\-ups

Mock\-ups are an increasingly essential resource for showing your ideas and the visual identity systems you create. In this list, I share with you some very good websites to download mock\-ups with which you can visualize your project:

 [Mr. Mockup](https://mrmockup.com/)
  
 A rich library of mock\-ups that has free and paid versions. Good combos to put together your projects, in high quality and warmth.

 [YellowImages](http://www.yellowimages.com/)
  
 A mock\-up factory that has everything you need, from a simple book mock\-up to an astronaut helmet. Of course, it is not free, but it will be essential in any project in which you need something very specific and you can incorporate that resource into the client's budget.

 [Pixeden](https://www.pixeden.com/)
  
 It offers general resources, including a catalogue of mock\-ups that is updated every month. You can access the free versions, but also other paid versions through a membership.
