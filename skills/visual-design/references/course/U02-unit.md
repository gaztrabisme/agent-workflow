- ### Magazine

[*De Stijl Magazine*](https://www.ubu.com/historical/de-stijl/index.html)
  
 Published between 1917 and 1931, it was a magazine dedicated to the visual arts. In it, Mondrian presented his theories on new artistic forms that he called "neoplasticism." This publication would bring together artists who agreed with the new avant\-garde that was emerging. and in it they discussed what modern art should be like, in the magazine they also published critiques, theories and manifestos. On the website that I leave you, you will find covers of this magazine, which have a very particular style.
- ### How to install a font

If you don't know how to install a font, don't panic, it's very simple:

 • In Windows, the simplest process is to double\-click on the .ttf file of the downloaded font and in the window that opens, click on the "Install" button so that it becomes part of the fonts on our computer.

 • On a Mac, double\-click on the .otf or .ttf file and a pop\-up window will appear containing all the font information. Simply click on "Install font" and it will automatically be added to your font catalog on Mac.

- ### Hexadecimal code

[HTML Color Codes](https://htmlcolorcodes.com/)
  
 In addition to CMYK and RGB modes, there is the hexadecimal color mode, based on RGB. This is the one you will see used in digital design programs (such as Figma). It is represented by the \# symbol followed by 6 numbers: \#FF0000 The first two symbols in the HTML color code represent the intensity of the color red. 00 is the least intense and FF is the most intense. The third and fourth numbers represent the intensity of green and the fifth and sixth numbers represent the intensity of black. blue. So, with this combination of red, green and blue intensities we can mix any color. If you want to know more, visit the HTML Color Codes website.
