# Unit 5 · The laws of visual perception: unity, weight, balance and movement

Anchor: 5642 · U5

Pepe Gimeno, designer and multidisciplinary artist, will teach you how to apply the laws of visual perception through works created with waste materials, fusing experimental graphics and art.

 This course will help you transform limitations into creative opportunities, applying what you have learned to posters, illustrations, layouts, typographic design or any graphic project.
