# Unit 4 · Composition techniques for graphic design

Anchor: 5642 · U4

Learn to create clear and attractive graphic structures by mastering the key elements of design: color, shape, space, balance and asymmetry.

 With Javier Alcaraz, an expert in editorial, branding and typography, you will discover how to articulate these components step by step to design impactful and relevant pieces that connect with your audience.

 Additionally, if you want to delve deeper into Adobe InDesign, explore their introductory courses or their automated editorial design specialization.

## Additional resources

- ### Composition and arrangement

["What is and how to use layout and composition"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gJhOHTOjGO8&feature=youtu.be)
  
 I recommend this interesting video from GCFLearnFree.org that explains principles for layout and composition in graphic design. You will see that it summarizes some of the points that we discuss here and also includes others that I consider within another category (for example, alignment seems to me to be a possibility of unity or similarity). The important thing is that, without intending to establish a list of definitive considerations, you feed yourself with different approaches to the problem of composition in design.
- ### Podcast

[Design Observer](https://soundcloud.com/designobserver)
  
 The work of great colleagues in the design world is a great source of inspiration. There is nothing better than hearing them explain their creative processes and compositional strategies firsthand. Guided by Jessica Helfand and Michael Bierut, the *Design Observer* podcast It is a good resource to understand how composition criteria are applied in real projects.

[Coolors](https://coolors.co/)

 A giant app developed by the leader in graphic design software. Its versatility allows you to use it very easily and to deepen its reach to very sophisticated levels. If you have a Creative Cloud account you can export your palettes directly to your applications.

Color applied to graphic design
  
 A very complete and inspiring course by Leire Fernández and Eduardo Herrera on communicative resources of color. Without a doubt, a semiotic complement to all the syntactic considerations we have seen in this course.
- ### Typographic classification

["Classification of types"](https://www.unostiposduros.com/clasificacion-de-los-tipos/)
  
 A more detailed typographic classification of classic and modern types can be found on the Unos Tipos duros website. Thanks to a more detailed description, visual evidence and recommended examples, you will be able to better understand the differences between typographic styles.

[*Practical Typography* \| Matthew Butterick](https://practicaltypography.com/)
