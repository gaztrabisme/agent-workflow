## Gate verdict

Record one verdict for each gate that was evaluated:

- PASS — the gate ran against the named artifact and its acceptance condition is met.
- FAIL — the gate ran and found an unmet condition, or required evidence is absent.
- WAIVED — an accountable decision owner approved a specific skip before evaluation; record approver, date, reason, affected scope, and accepted risk.
- If review execution is DEFERRED, do not assign a verdict or claim completion for the dependent judgment.

## Findings

Use one row per finding. Link to the precise artifact location or source evidence.

| ID | Severity | Defect | Evidence | Effect on use or decision | Fix / disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- High — threatens correctness, safety, result integrity, or a required success criterion; resolve before shipping or completion.
- Med — materially weakens use or a decision, but has a bounded workaround.
- Low — limited clarity or polish issue without a material effect on the decision.
