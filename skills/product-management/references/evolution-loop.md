# Evolution-log use for product-management

Use this note only to keep this skill's `EVOLUTION.md` legible and tied to real product-management use. The Evolution skill owns later trace-based changes and settled verdicts.

1. Record which product briefs, review comments, or decisions were observed and what was outside the evidence.
2. Separate repeated patterns from one-off observations. Turn a proposed change into a hypothesis with an expected result and an evidence floor.
3. Apply the smallest change that addresses the evidence, preserving the approved scope and source-backed practices.
4. Keep the verdict `PENDING` until the evidence floor is met. Then record whether the hypothesis is kept, revised, or reverted, with the observed evidence and remaining limits.
