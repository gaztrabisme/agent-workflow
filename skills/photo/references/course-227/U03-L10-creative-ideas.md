## Creative prompts for getting started

Anchor: 227 · U3 · L10 · 00:00–01:02

Now that we have taken all kinds of photographs and know how to use the phone to capture them, let’s look at other creative ideas that can help you get past the block when you start a photograph and have no idea where to begin or what to do. Here are several ideas. Some are hashtags and some are not. They are creative ideas people take part in across the Instagram community. They can help you unlock your creativity and communicate with other Instagram users who are trying them too.

The first has no hashtag, but it is something people do throughout the community: play with scale (*escala*). Introduce an element in the foreground (*primer plano*). For example, while taking a photograph with your phone, put your hand at this distance and use it to play with something far away in the background. Andrew Cutler, the man I’m showing you here, is a master at this. He does it all the time and does it very well.

## Blur on purpose

Anchor: 227 · U3 · L10 · 01:02–01:38

Another idea is Blur on Purpose: deliberately taking an out-of-focus photograph. You do this by focusing on a very close point. When I want to do this, I put my hand close to the phone, focus on my hand, and lock the focus there by holding it down, as we have seen. Then I remove my hand and take the photograph wherever I want, and everything comes out blurred.

These photographs look good when the tones in the landscape, the scene at home, or whatever you are photographing are beautiful, and the light is beautiful too. That way you get bokeh, the little dots of out-of-focus light. All of that looks great.

## Portraits from behind and from where you stand

Anchor: 227 · U3 · L10 · 01:38–02:15

Another one is Backs Feel (captions: *Bugs Feel*). I love it, although I do not know why: take portraits of people from behind. It gives the image a mysterious feel. As you can see, you take a portrait as you normally would, with whatever props or in any scene, but you do not show the person’s face.

From Where I Stand is a photograph you take as soon as you open the phone camera, with your feet below. The creative part begins when you start taking it in very different places, perhaps in the middle of a forest or with your dog in front of you, like this woman. Any kind of idea could work; the limit is what you can think of.

## Photographing a photographer and jumping

Anchor: 227 · U3 · L10 · 02:55–03:27

Caughtgramming (captions: *Cogtraming*) is simply taking a photograph of someone who is taking an Instagram photograph. Usually these are split between a picture of the phone itself taking the Instagram photo and one of the person who is taking it directly, showing the whole person, the setting, and so on. This one is very famous.

Jumpstagram (captions: *Jamstagram*) is simply jumping. We have all done it at some point, jumping for a photograph. Now you just have to find the way you want to jump; that is what will give you a different way of doing this hashtag.

## Walking through a scene and Sleeveface

Anchor: 227 · U3 · L10 · 03:27–04:34

Stride By (captions: *Stripe By*) shows people walking in profile along a façade or against a background. You can do it in thousands of ways, with people very far away and very small, or closer, with beautiful backgrounds that draw attention. The idea is to choose a setting you would love to photograph on its own and humanize it a little, instead of making the typical photograph of just a house.

Finally, this one is not very popular, but I love it and think it is very creative. Sleeveface (captions: *Sleek Face*) is when people use vinyl records whose designs show people’s faces or even hands, and integrate them into the photograph to replace that part of themselves. If the record has a face on it, I put it right in front of me and line it up so the perspective makes it look like my face. I hope you feel like trying these ideas, and please share them with us so we can see what your imagination has come up with. Now that we have all kinds of photographs, creative ones and ones from everyday life, we are going to edit them.
