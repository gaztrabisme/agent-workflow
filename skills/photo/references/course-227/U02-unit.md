# Unit 2 · Before the photo

Anchor: 227 · U2

In this unit we will look at everything you need to take into account before taking a photo. It's important that you write down these tips and try to put them into practice everyday, so that you don't forget them in the moment of truth!

## Tasks

- ### Getting your materials

Before continuing with the rest of the lessons, it will be useful to have the materials that we talked about. You can find where to buy these materials in the additional resources. 

![](https://assets7.domestika.org/course-images/000/002/507/2507-big.png)
- ### Complete your Instagram profile

Before you start uploading your photos to Instagram, you need to have an account and complete your profile. But don't go crazy! I recommend you really think about what you are going to write and how you are going to make it attractive. Take a look at Instagram profiles that inspire you and those that we have been sharing in the forum. How do they describe themselves? Do you add emojis to your text? Do you add your contact information? Once it's clear how you want to move forward, set up your account using the information from your projects.

## Additional resources

- ### Where to buy materials

**Cardboard backdrops.** They are bought in fine art or stationary stores. If in your city there is a faculty of fine arts, a faculty of architecture or a drawing academy, call them and ask where the students buy their materials. 
  
 **Pen box.** Normally they have in all the stationeries. If they have varying thicknesses, choose the finest so it won't weigh too much. 
  
 **Washi tape.** I usually buy this from online craft stores. My favorite brand is MT (Masking Tape). If you can't find it, you can use perfectly bodybuilder tape, which is used by wall painters. 
  
 **Blue Tack.** They usually have it in bargain stores, stationery or DIY stores. It's important that you always buy it white! The blue can stain. My preferred brand is iMedio. 
  
 **Props.** In the next unit we will talk more about the props and I'll tell you where I usually buy them.
