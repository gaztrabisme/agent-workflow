I’ll surely leave some out; I don’t know, there may be a thousand more that I like, but these are great for explaining what I want to explain now. As you can see, each one has its own particular aesthetic and its own way of approaching Instagram. Some give more importance to text; others give more importance to photographs, or simply make a collage of different things they want to show. But they all have unity and homogeneity in their aesthetics. In the next lesson, we’ll start by looking at the materials we’ll use throughout the course.
