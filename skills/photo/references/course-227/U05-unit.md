# Unit 5 · Instagram Stories

Anchor: 227 · U5

Instagram Stories is an Instagram tool that lets you share photos and videos up to 15 seconds for 24 hours. Currently it is one of the most popular features of Instagram and is generally looked at more than normal posts. In this unit I am going to show you how I usually take photos for Instagram Stories and how to use Stories in the application itself. 

Currently, you can add your Stories to the Featured Stories section of your profile, and that way they can always be accessed (and not disappear after 24 hours). Featured stories appear in the circles just below your profile description.
