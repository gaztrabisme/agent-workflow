## Tasks

- ### Make a moodboard 
Practice exercise 3\.1

A moodboard is an inspiration board that gathers that many elements that will make up your photographic style. It is a great tool to inspire and to always consider when creating your project. 

If you can not think of ideas when you go to choose your colors, your props or your background, it can be great to see the theme of 'Ideas for your project'. This topic is creating a brainstorming collaborative (or brainstorm).

All students can participate and get ideas for their projects!

 To do so, follow these steps: 

 01\_ Think about the values you want to convey. 

 02\_ Define a color palette that you like, and that represents these values. 

 03\_ Gather all kinds of images: photographs, illustrations, film frames etc... that are related to your project and that you visually love. You can use a folder, take screenshots, save to Pinterest etc.. 

04\_ Choose some of the images you have chosen and join them in a moodboard with any app, be it Canva, Photoshop or any other app where you can make collages. You can also print them and make a collage by hand!

 You can see some moodboards we have created at Melon Blanc for our clients, in the attached documents of this unit.

- ### Gather your props

Having created a moodboard, it will be much easier to find which props suit you. Print out your moodboard and get searching for your props. My advice is to look at home first (or those of your relatives ) and once you have gathered everything you can without having to buy anything, go to stores or shop online to complete your collection. 

Anyway, don't stress if you only have a few things! A prop collection is something that you will create little by little, adding to when you can. The main thing is to always keep an eye out for new things, so that don't miss out on key items.
- ### Take your first picture 
Practice exercise 3\.2

Choose one of the three types of photographs we have taken in this unit and get to work! Use your moodboard and your materials and get to work on your first photo, always keeping your project in mind. 

It's best if you show us the photo unedited, so we'll see how that same photo improves in the next unit. 

I also recommend you document the entire process in the final project, so take photos of how you assemble your props etc.. 

Before getting down to work and doing any practice, it can be great to go through the topic Frequent mistakes 

In this topic there will be an index with the MOST COMMON ERRORS of all the students and a lot of extra tips that will help you improve your photos.

- ### Stores where to buy props

As you will see in the course, the props you will need for your project will be totally unique and will be related to your specific project. Anyway, I leave you here a list with the stores where I usually go to look for props when I start a project, I hope they help you!

[Vaivén](https://vaivenmultibrand.com/)
  
Online store specializing in decoration and stationery. I love her with all my heart and I can't stop buying props from her, as they are such special products, they always make the photo beautiful!

[H\&M Home](https://www2.hm.com/es_es/home.html)
  
They have physical and online store, lots of great decor and textiles (it's perfect for cushions, sheets, etc.). The best thing is that if you don't like something, you can return it at any H\&M store.

[Zara Home](https://www.zarahome.com/es/)
  
They have a physical store and online. It is a perfect option for plates, cups, spoons and all kinds of decoration. I also usually shop here for cosmetic photo towels. My favorite trick is to put in the search engine the colors of the project I'm photographing and find lots of props already sorted by color.

[Notable Notebooks](https://www.notable-notebooks.com/)
  
Online stationery store. Great for those who want to take pictures of their desk. My second favorite after Vaivén.

[Desenio](https://desenio.es/)
  
It's an online store for prints, although I never really buy prints here! What I definitely recommend are their frames, for me they are the best and I've been using them for years.

[El Corte Inglés](https://www.elcorteingles.es/)
  
It seems that it is complicated to find props here, but I always surprise myself by finding basic objects, very neutral but very nice, both in the tableware area and in decoration.

[Nordic Nest](https://www.nordicnest.es/)
  
It is quite a bit more expensive because it has designer objects, but once in a while it is great to get something very special that gives an extra point of personality to the photos.

[Smallable](https://www.smallable.com/es)
  
In theory it's children's, but it has all kinds of decorating stuff really. It's usually pretty expensive too, though it's great for more special items.
