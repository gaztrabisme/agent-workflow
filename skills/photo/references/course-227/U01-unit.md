# Unit 1 · Introduction

Anchor: 227 · U1

Hello everyone and welcome! Before I start, I would to say that I am very, very happy that this course is finally a reality and be able to help the many people who want to grow on social media and to have you as my students. You can't imagine what it means for me to be your teacher! 

Also I am available in the forum to answer any questions or doubts your may have. I looking forward to meeting all of you! In this first unit I will tell you a little about myself and my work as a product photographer at Melon Blanc. I will also show you some of my favorite instagrammers.

## Tasks

- ### Teach us your favorite instagrammers 
Practice exercise 1\.2

Tell us what your favorite Instagram accounts are and why. Keep in mind, it's especially important to explain why. That way, little by little, we will have a more critical view. Take the opportunity to get inspired by the favorite instagrammers of the rest of the students and share your inspirations with others.
