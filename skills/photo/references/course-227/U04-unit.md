# Unit 4 · Editing a photo

Anchor: 227 · U4

We have reached one of the most enjoyable units. When creating a photo, the edition can be just as important as the shot. A good edition can turn a normal photograph into a professional photo! I'm going to teach you how to edit using my favorite applications.

## Tasks

- ### Edit your photo 
Practice exercise 4\.1

Choose one of the previously mentioned applications to edit the photo you took in unit 3\. If you have any questions, remember to ask me in the forum. Once it is edited, share it with the group! I'll give you my feedback and help you improve it in any way you might need. 

Before getting down to work and doing any practice, it can be great to go through the topic Frequent mistakes In this topic there will be an index with the MOST COMMON ERRORS of all the students and a lot of extra tips that will help you improve your photos.

IMPORTANT. If you have any questions, read this forum topic so I can solve it as soon as possible: New forum operation.
- ### Sort your photos in Later

When programming photos in Later, the Preview tool is very helpful to get a sneak peek at how your instagram gallery will look. Whenever I'm going to publish a photo, I check out how it will look next to the other posts around it. There are many strategies on how to order them: by color (to create a rainbow?), by order (making sure they don't seem too similar to other photos next to it) and many more. Find the way you like best, start practicing and you will improve daily.
