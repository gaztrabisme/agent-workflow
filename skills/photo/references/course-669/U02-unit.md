# Unit 2 · Get ready

Anchor: 669 · U2

Here we will see everything you need to start your photo shoot. I will teach you the importance of creating a link with the models you work with, through conversation, so that your portrait acquires depth. 
  
 Also, I will explain how to choose your locations and the team to work with.

## Tasks

- ### Listen to yourself

Think about what situations you feel most comfortable, consider what you think your photographic routine might be. How much and how do you want to interact with the model regarding the session: if you will see each other directly on the day of the photos or you will be before, in what space do you want to be, doing what, how long you expect to be ...
- ### move on

Think about what you want to achieve and look for a space that allows it. Be Creative / a. 
  
 Also choose the time of day when you think you will have the right light.
- ### Choose

Choose the material and accessories that will allow you to take the pictures you imagine.

## Additional resources

- ### Discover which equipment that suits your needs

If you have doubts about which material is better or which one to buy, it has always been great for me to ask in this [forum](https://www.canonistas.com/) . There are people who know a lot about it and are attentive 24/7\.
- ### Find your locations

[Passporter](https://passporterapp.com/) is a very useful application to discover new locations, either in your own city or taking advantage of a trip. Users hang their photographs and the exact location of the place. And it's free!
