# Unit 1 · Introduction

In this unit I will tell you who I am and how I got here: when I began to be interested in photography, what I was looking for and what I am still looking for. 
  
 We will also see some of the references that have marked me the most and I will explain what we are going to do in this course.

## Additional resources

- ### Other photographers who inspire me

Here you have the names of other photographers with whom I connect emotionally: 

 1\. Philippe Halsman 
  
 With his project *[Jumpology](https://www.magnumphotos.com/arts-culture/philippe-halsman-jump-book/)* portrayed the greatest public figures of the time jumping. From President Nixon to Marilyn Monroe. 
  
 *"When you ask a person to jump, his attention goes above all to the act of jumping and leaves the mask: the real person appears."* 

 2\. Francesca Woodman 
  
 It has always been disturbing to me. A girl too young to be so sad. His photographs manifest to me as a question mark whose answer is in the inner world of Francesca, lost forever after life was taken off at age 23\. 

 3\. Petra Collins 
  
 [@petrafcollins](https://www.instagram.com/petrafcollins/?hl=es)
  
 With only 26 years he has managed to create a new imaginary through its colors and elements. I find it fascinating in every way.
