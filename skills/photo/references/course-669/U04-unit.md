# Unit 4 · Editing

Anchor: 669 · U4

Now, I'm going to show you how to edit your photos with *Lightroom* and how I choose the final photographs. 
  
 In addition, I will give you tips to spread your work.

## Tasks

- ### Shoot the arrow

Think about where you want to go and make a list of what you need to get it. From the most insignificant things to the greatest. Start with the small ones and get to it with patience but without pause. You will not be able to hit it at once, doing things right is a job of ants that are progressing little by little.
- ### Get out of your comfort zone

It is very easy to get stuck in a way to edit colors, lights, or to photograph, and in doing so we do not advance. Ask yourself what you do less, find ways to turn the way you work, reinvent yourself every day without losing your essence. This will be the best way to move forward and find you.
- ### Become a professional account 
Practice exercise 4\.1

An important part of building your identity as a photographer or photographer in these times is to open an Instagram account, which serves as a portfolio and for people and brands to find you easily. In this way you will also have to do the exercise of selecting with which works of yours you identify more and placing them in a manner consistent with the rest.
