## Preparing the photo session

Anchor: 669 · U1 · L03 · 00:00–00:37

In this lesson, I’ll explain what we’ll do in this course. We’ll start with the photographic process (*proceso fotográfico*): the conversation with the model, what we discuss before the photo session, how we interact, and what we’re looking for. Then we’ll move on to the location (*localización*): which spaces to choose and why, and above all, when to shoot—what time of day. We’ll also cover the equipment (*material*): what equipment we use in each situation during a photo session.

## Practising indoors with windows and found materials

Anchor: 669 · U1 · L03 · 00:37–01:14

Then we’ll move on to practice. We’ll have a photo session together, and I’ll show you how to take photographs indoors, including by the windows of an apartment, and how we can make the most of those windows. We’ll also try to make the most of the inside of a house without it serving as a direct light source. What can we do inside a house or an apartment? We’ll also play with more artistic elements, such as glass and plastic—things we might find anywhere. We’ll see how to get the most out of them. Then, editing.

## Editing and sharing the photographs

Anchor: 669 · U1 · L03 · 01:14–01:38

We’ll edit the photographs we’ve taken with Lightroom. I’ll show you how I edit them and which tools I use to achieve this distinctive style. Finally, we’ll look at how to circulate our work once we have photographs we’re proud of: what to do with them and how to achieve our goals as photographers.
