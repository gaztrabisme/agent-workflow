## Adjust the arm shapes with Liquify

Anchor: 1343 · U56 · L86 · 05:36–08:52

To me the arms have a slightly odd shape. One is very tight and the other is broader, so I will even them out to keep one from drawing more attention than the other. I create a composite layer, call it Liquify (captions: “Equify”), and convert it to a Smart Object so I can edit it later if needed. There is a little bump here, so I start with the Forward Warp tool at fairly low pressure, perhaps around five, and push it inward. Then I use the Push Left tool, again at a low setting, with a slightly larger brush. Three seemed quite good. I paint in the opposite direction to push those pixels to the right, then use Forward Warp to reshape it slightly.

One thing that bothers me about the Liquify workspace is that I cannot see the edge of the image properly. I can see the edge, but I cannot move the frame outside it to get a clear view, and sometimes we make mistakes. It would be easier to see the whole thing. The only way is to zoom out, but I want to zoom in to see the edge clearly. I am almost done, but I want to push it a little more. It is quite rare to widen arms instead of slimming them, so it is refreshing to do the opposite. On the other arm, I will slim it a little. Now the arms feel more even, and I will change the bottom angle a little.
