# Select keepers: choose images before investing in detailed edits

**Reader:** the photographer editing a session or social-image set. **Scope:** build a deliberate working set and avoid retouching near-duplicates that add nothing.

**Source lessons:** [669 U4 L10–L11](../course-669/U04-L11-selection.md), [227 U4 L11](../course-227/U04-L11-editing-photos-with-snapseed.md).

## Use two selection passes

The courses disagree about timing. The Instagram course marks favorites before editing and edits one representative image of each type. The portrait course exports edited candidates, compares them by space and revisits the shortlist after time away. (227 · U4 · L11 · 00:08–01:38; 669 · U4 · L10 · 15:06–17:56; 669 · U4 · L11 · 00:00–03:14; 669 · U4 · L11 · 03:14–06:44; 669 · U4 · L11 · 08:22–12:37; 669 · U4 · L11 · 12:37–15:44)

**Derived workflow:** first reject technical misses and mark promising frames before detailed retouch; after base edits, compare the finalists again and let them rest before a consequential final selection. This preserves the early-edit efficiency of course 227 and the deliberate late comparison in course 669. (derived from 227 · U4 · L11 · 00:08–01:38; 669 · U4 · L11 · 08:22–12:37; 669 · U4 · L11 · 12:37–15:44)

Compare near-duplicates by expression, gaze, gesture, detail, focus, background, and whether each viewpoint adds something. The portrait teacher compares smoke clarity, eye definition, hand and shoulder placement, and a gesture that is still developing. (669 · U4 · L11 · 00:00–03:14; 669 · U4 · L11 · 03:14–06:44; 669 · U4 · L11 · 06:44–08:22; 669 · U4 · L11 · 08:22–12:37)

| Pass | Keep | Set aside |
|---|---|---|
| Working shortlist | Requested subjects, usable focus/exposure, varied moments | Misses, accidental duplicates, frames that fail the brief |
| Final selection | Images with a distinct expression, viewpoint, or story role | Near-duplicates that add no visible difference |

## Check before moving on

T3 passes when every selected image has a clear purpose in the set and the requested subjects/moments are represented. Course 669's 216-to-63-to-fewer demonstration is an example, not a general quota. (669 · U4 · L11 · 00:00–03:14; 669 · U4 · L11 · 12:37–15:44)

**Practice the teacher sets:** review a first selection, return after a pause, and identify the images that still matter. See [Unit 4](../course-669/U04-unit.md).
