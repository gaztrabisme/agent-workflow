# Retouch and composite: make only the changes the brief permits

**Reader:** the editor making local portrait corrections, feature changes, or composites. **Scope:** choose a contained method, preserve useful photographic detail, and keep edits reversible where possible.

**Source lessons:** [1343 U19 L23–L26](../course-1343/U19-L23-selections-1.md), [1343 U20 L27–L29](../course-1343/U20-L27-layer-masks-1.md), [1343 U21 L30](../course-1343/U21-L30-blending-images-together.md), [1343 U36 L56–L57](../course-1343/U36-L56-cloning-and-working-with-adjustment-layers-1.md), [1343 U37–U41](../course-1343/U37-L58-correcting-and-enhancing-skin-tones.md), [1343 U44 L67](../course-1343/U44-L67-retouching-a-portrait.md), [1343 U46 L69](../course-1343/U46-L69-making-a-road-map-to-plan-edit.md), [1343 U49–U52](../course-1343/U49-L72-dodge-and-burn-1.md).

## Build composites from matched source material

The Photoshop course introduces image blending with reduced layer opacity or a gradient layer mask (1343 · U21 · L30 · 00:00–01:07). In its practice composite, the teacher positions the source image (1343 · U22 · L31 · 00:00–02:36), matches brightness and color (1343 · U22 · L31 · 02:36–04:05), then selects and refines a mask (1343 · U22 · L31 · 04:05–05:59; 1343 · U22 · L31 · 05:59–08:15). A global adjustment layer is added to tie the elements together (1343 · U22 · L31 · 08:58–09:59). Screen is a blend mode for additive light (1343 · U21 · L30 · 03:42–06:33); blend modes can be a quick starting point without necessarily improving the image (1343 · U21 · L30 · 06:33–09:55).

**Derived retouch order:** solve large scene problems and local cleanup before small feature polish; finish with a global unifying adjustment. The course shows different image-specific routes, so this is an efficient synthesis, not a fixed teacher recipe. ## Failures

**Problem: retouch that removes the subject's identity.** The course says blur can make skin look plastic (1343 · U41 · L63 · 10:49–12:52) and that excessive softening can look fake (1343 · U41 · L63 · 12:53–15:57). It warns that a copied tooth can look duplicated and reveal the retouch (1343 · U52 · L78 · 09:38–10:27). Some Face-Aware Liquify changes can look unnatural (1343 · U50 · L75 · 08:02–10:18); the teacher advises approval for face-shape changes (1343 · U50 · L75 · 10:18–12:42) and using Liquify sparingly (1343 · U50 · L75 · 12:42–13:05). The field rules set the identity protections and distinguish local edits from whole-image reconstruction (field rules FR-03–FR-05).

**Fix:** return to the untouched original, reduce or remove the offending edit, restore local texture and shape, then compare at full-frame and close-up scale.

**Practice the teacher sets:** plan and retouch the portrait in Unit 44; choose the needed techniques and share a before-and-after for feedback. See [Unit 44](../course-1343/U44-unit.md).
