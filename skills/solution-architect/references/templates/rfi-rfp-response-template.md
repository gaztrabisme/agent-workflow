## Response coverage rules

- Match the client's section order and numbering, replace every placeholder, delete guidance notes before submission, and add a presentation commitment only when required.
- Boil the intended outcome to one concise audience focused message and use it to test the executive summary [book:storytelling-with-data], The 3‐minute story & Big Idea.
- Identify what the evaluator needs to know or do first, then select data that directly supports that case [book:storytelling-with-data], Who, what, and how: illustrated by example.
- Choose each diagram to answer a question and treat it as a purpose bound model alongside the prose [book:software-architect-elevator], Diagrams Are Models.
- Choose viewpoints for relevant concerns and use a checklist to catch gaps without filling every view by rote [book:software-architect-elevator], Viewpoints.
- Filter options through the client’s domain drivers and specific context before comparing the important trade-offs [book:software-architecture-hard-parts], Model Relevant Domain Cases.
- State a recommended option or hybrid, why alternatives lost, and design-time checks before commitment [book:facilitating-software-architecture], Consequences.
- Work with stakeholders to identify the system’s key quality attributes and constraints, then tailor measurements to those priorities [book:software-architecture-metrics], Quality attribute requirements.
- Answer each client requirement in prose or a table [book:hbr-guide-business-writing], ***Tailor your message***.
- Follow the client's pricing format and answer every pricing question or explicitly defer it to a labelled attachment; match precision to RFI or RFP.
- Include the presentation/demo section only when invited or mandated, and confirm format and the right business and technical attendees.
- Make the compliance and traceability appendix account for every requirement and map it to solution, technology, and phase.
- Use one traceability row per RFP requirement; group RFI requirements only if every reference remains accounted for.
- When the client's compliance table is mismatched, state your mapping convention instead of asking permission.
