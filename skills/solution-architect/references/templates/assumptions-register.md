## Assumption pairing

- The book describes a project-long RAID record that includes assumptions; making it the primary always-current artifact for every unconfirmed dependency is the rule’s extension [book:semantic-software-design], RAID.
- Pair each load-bearing client question with a fallback assumption and copy it into this register.
- Keep schedule-stopping client deliverables in the obligation register with a deadline and named focal point, not in assumptions.
- The book records an assumption’s basis and validation action, and allows a remediation if it proves false; confidence, linked question, and confirming party are additions [book:semantic-software-design], RAID.
- Surface high-impact, low-confidence assumptions in the client response in a correctable form.
