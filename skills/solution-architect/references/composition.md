# Composition and Handoffs

Use these boundaries to consume presale inputs and transfer signed decisions. Qualification and account intelligence stay with their owner; signed-client control moves to delivery.

## Signature handoff

- At signature, turn accepted decisions into build inputs and record what binds the build.
- At signature, pass the estimate, roadmap, acceptance annex, client obligations, risks, and ID spine to delivery as its baseline.

## Workshop inputs

- Carry accepted workshop use cases, journey maps, and ROI baselines into architecture; reuse platform choices when that platform is being positioned.

## Inputs from account intelligence

- MEDDICC links the economic buyer’s focus to organizational strategic objectives; this rule combines that lens with the BI profile and political context [book:meddicc], ECONOMIC BUYER.
- Build positioning around a teaching insight that leads to genuine strengths, challenges customer assumptions, and prompts action [book:challenger-sale], NOT JUST ANY TEACHING. *COMMERCIAL* TEACHING.

## Inputs from discovery work

- Use discovery-scored use cases, journey maps, and ROI baseline in SA Phases 2–3.
- For workshop-originated deals, reuse discovery commercial framing and stack guidance rather than deriving them again.

## Distinguish account intelligence from business intelligence

- The book’s handoff practice is to share the selected decision with its implementers before implementation, without defining the broader artifact bundle or exclusions [book:facilitating-software-architecture], Step 2c (optional): Sharing your decision.
- Give the pre-signature PM a task-and-discipline WBS with effort empty; strip cut order, risk narrative, and scope defence.
- Give the commercial or bid owner cost drivers, envelope, and exclusions in a priced attachment; strip architecture detail.
- Shape the story around the evaluator and the action they need to take; audience and action should guide the narrative [book:storytelling-with-data], Constructing the story.
- At signature, give delivery the estimate, roadmap, acceptance annex, obligations, risks, and ID spine as its full baseline.
- Give the pre-signature PM ownership of release split and staffing; enumerate work and leave effort pricing to the disciplines.
- The book calls for decision records to carry context and alternative analysis; it does not prescribe the timing or contents of a developer handoff [book:fundamentals-enterprise-architecture], Architecture Decision Deliverable.
- Treat NFRs as prioritized design constraints; the book does not define a developer handoff or design-gate checklist [book:fundamentals-enterprise-architecture], Summarizing NFRs.
- When the deal is won or approved and the build begins, hand off from SA to dev/Design [book:facilitating-software-architecture], Step 2c (optional): Sharing your decision.
- Treat a client request for Business Intelligence or Power BI as an architectural capability, not as a trigger for the business-intelligence research skill.
