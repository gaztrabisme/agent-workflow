# Executive Profiling

Treat a profile as a working hypothesis. Titles vary, and an externally hired leader may not inherit the relationship or priorities associated with the predecessor; check roles and reactions in discovery.

## Political scenarios to test

An executive may own an in-flight build that overlaps the proposed work; that can create a real incentive conflict even when the meeting remains cordial. Treat it as a hypothesis, verify the project and decision criteria directly, and do not infer motive from title or tenure. [book:articulating-design-decisions], Identifying Influencers

For a new executive, an inherited vendor list may be reconsidered. Ask about prior employers and solutions they have used; keep vendor preference and personal goals open until the person or reliable evidence confirms them. [book:meddicc], ## DISCOVERY
