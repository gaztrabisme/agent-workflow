# Defensive Positioning

A separate negative-space table cannot make a one-sided synthesis balanced. Carry relevant counterevidence and contradictions into the main account of the finding. Use a working table such as: proposed statement | evidence and limits | room-safe response.

## Working formats

| Proposed statement | Evidence and limits | Response for the room |
|---|---|---|
| [claim] | [verified facts, source, date, uncertainty] | [accurate, bounded wording] |

| Client need | Current evidence | Fit, gap, or open question |
|---|---|---|
| [need] | [what the sources establish] | [what can responsibly be said] |

## Bounded answers in practice

For example, describe an integration path through its actual gateway rather than implying a direct connection. State deployment status without exposing a confidential client, and name an untested platform as a validation item for a pilot. The useful answer separates what works, what has been demonstrated, and what remains unknown.
