# Stakeholder Mapping

A contact list can hide coverage gaps. Track people by role and relationship as well as by name, then test who influences, approves, uses, or can block the decision.

## Coverage check

Review both the people named in the meeting and the wider decision group. Unknown role or influence is a research question, not a blank to fill from a title.
