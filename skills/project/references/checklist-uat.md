# Checklist test

How each line of [the issue checklist](issue-checklist.md) is itself tested, so a line that cannot catch its defect, or can be passed by a wrong artifact, is found before a real plan depends on it.

## Method

Each checklist line is a quality criterion, and each needs a quality method: how it is checked. PRINCE2's product description records, for every product, its quality criteria, quality tolerance (the acceptable range), quality method (for example inspection or review) and quality responsibilities (producer, reviewer, approver). [book:prince2-managing-successful-projects], A. Product description outlines

The review is done by someone who did not produce the product, against the criteria in its description; the chair of a quality review must be independent of the product. [book:prince2-managing-successful-projects], 8.4. Technique: recommended quality review technique. Here that is a reviewer agent with no prior context.

## Tests per line

1. **Detection.** A seeder plants the line's broken example in a WBS workbook or plan note. A reviewer agent with no prior context receives the artifact and the checklist, nothing else. The line passes if the reviewer finds the defect and names the line number. Plant one defect per copy, so each finding maps to one line.
2. **Gaming.** The reviewer is then given the line and asked to build an artifact that passes it as written but is still wrong. If it succeeds, the line is rewritten so the gamed artifact fails, and both tests are run again.

The first run (14 Oct 2026) is recorded in the skill's [EVOLUTION.md](../EVOLUTION.md).

The WBS template's negative fixtures ([templates/wbs](../templates/wbs/README.md), `tests/fixtures/`) hold broken workbooks the verifier must name; a seeder can start from them.

## Checks by line

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find the visible cell that recomputes the owner's effort total from their detail rows with their method, and the cell that recomputes their cost total with their cost rules; confirm the cost recomputation was made before any new pricing; compare each with their stated figure; check any mismatch is in the question list | No recomputation cell, a cost recomputation made after new rates were applied, or a mismatch corrected instead of asked | The reproduction prices one role at our rate instead of the owner's, and the note says "reproduced" | The recomputation cell references the owner's stated total, or a factor is tuned until it matches; check the cell's precedents are detail rows |
| 2 | On the bridge: start equals the owner's total, end equals ours, the lines sum exactly to the difference; each line lists row IDs that exist and a reason about those rows; any cut made to meet a price is its own line | Bridge does not close, a line has no rows or a generic reason, or a price cut is merged into another line | The lines sum to less than the gap; one line reads "estimate refinement" and names no rows | One "other adjustments" line lists many rows to absorb the remainder, or a reason that names the rows but would fit any row |

| 18 | Count legends (one only); list every fill used on every sheet, conditional formatting included; each appears in the legend with one meaning | Two legends, a fill with two meanings, or a fill missing from the legend | Changed figures marked in the same yellow as fill-in cells; a conditional red not in the legend | One legend entry giving one colour two meanings ("fill in or changed"), or two near-identical fills listed separately that no reader can tell apart |
| 19 | A review record for this version names a reviewer who did not see the drafting, a different one each round; the brief given to them holds the artifact and its quality criteria, not the drafter's conclusions; findings and the action on each are recorded; findings still open after the third round are recorded as sent to the PM | No review, a review of an earlier version, a brief carrying conclusions, or open findings after round three not escalated | The review record predates the last change; the brief says which parts are already fine | The drafter's conclusions placed inside the artifact (a notes sheet explaining why each figure is right), or the round count reset by calling it a new review |

## Limits found in the first run

The first run (14 Oct 2026) found that a line catches a defect it is shown but can still be passed by labelling (every row "medium", a reason that sounds specific), by omission (a dependency never registered, no exclusion list) or by a motive the reviewer cannot see (a testing cut given a reason other than price). Rewording did not remove these: a second gaming pass on the rewritten lines still passed 9 of 20. What closes them is evidence, not wording: re-derive figures instead of reading labels, and compare with the owner's file, the last issued version and the price target. Details in [EVOLUTION.md](../EVOLUTION.md), Evolution 6.
