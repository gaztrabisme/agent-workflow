# Anti-patterns

The hard no-list for this skill. Planning items come from the former `project-management` skill; running items from the former `conductor` skill. Each item is stated once, here.

## Planning

- Fill estimates or decide the release cut for the person who owns those decisions.
- Use the same WBS hierarchy or visualization for every project.
- Hide overrun, dependencies, or capacity gaps behind a tidy summary.
- Keep regenerating a workbook after its recipient has taken over hand editing.
- Turn the general plan into a signed-engagement tracker.
- Cut an estimate to reach a price and record the cut as productivity. Show it as a price decision on the bridge.
- Apply one assumption twice, such as a per-task AI saving and a flat AI factor on the same total.
- Plan to recover a loss by loading price onto unrelated change requests.
- Build a supplier concession (free work, a lower rate, a cut below the formula) into the estimate as scope. It is a decision for the person who sets the price, shown with its amount.
- Report only the minimum duration. Show options with team size, end date and slack.

## Running agents

- Doing the unit in the coordinator: writing the deliverable, reasoning about the domain, or absorbing work refused by every lane.
- Smuggling upstream content into a brief instead of naming its exact file, section, and row.
- Treating a lane's completion claim as acceptance, or recording PASS without reading an output sample.
- Rewording a failed criterion to fit what was produced.
- Leaving a foreseeable fork out of `DECIDE NOW`, or returning mid-run with a context-free question.
- Launching fan-out without a declared meeting point, or silently smoothing away contradictory evidence.
- Skipping the project and source read before planning.
- Planning on a guessed intent when the outcome is open, or interviewing about what the record already settles or about how to build it.
- Waiving a sibling's gate to meet the plan.
- Adding process elements that do not earn their cost, or omitting checks that matter to the task's risk. Scale the Plan Block and artifacts to the work rather than using a fixed unit-count trigger. [book:pmbok-guide-7e], 3.3.2. Processes.
