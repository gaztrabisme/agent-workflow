When a criterion is Blocked, create a corresponding obligation row. Record each overdue dependency and its concrete effect; do not absorb client-caused schedule delay without a dated record (5907 · U3 · L24 · 03:10–04:20). At close, summarize which dependencies slipped and by how much.

For overdue or approaching dates, use automated notices when available to supplement the tracker. The delivery lead records the date and evidence of a written follow-up; communication owns the audience-ready wording. [book:project-management-with-ai], Reducing errors in reporting with AI (5907 · U3 · L24 · 03:10–04:20)

The cycle report always includes an Awaiting Client section, even when it has no open rows. For each open item, show owner, due date, overdue days and concrete impact instead of a vague delay label.
