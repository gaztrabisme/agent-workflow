# Unit 2 · Digital Product Design for Humans: Lean UX

Anchor: 1490 · U2

In this unit we are going to know the basic concepts to design a digital product in the 21st century, at the end of the lesson you will have the theoretical bases so that your designs are perfectly "usable" and a work methodology based on the Lean philosophy that we will use at throughout the course.

## Tasks

- ### Create your project Kanban board

From now on we are going to manage all the exercises of the course with a Kanban board:

 **1\.** Access [Trello](https://trello.com/) to create a free account. (You can also use the Notion template that I have left you in resources or use sticky notes on a table or a wall, as long as it does not have gotelé.)

 **2\.** Create the following columns:

 to. Inbox:
  
 Here we will include on cards all the exercises, links, references, etc. (It's a kind of "mixed bag".)

 b. **To do** (To do):
  
 Here we will include the different tasks and activities that we have to perform, prioritized from top (most important or immediate) to bottom (those that we can do later).

 c. **Doing** (Doing) (3\):
  
 Here we will include the tasks / exercises that we are going to do throughout the day, we will not have more than 3 in this column. Until we finish one and move it to "Done" we will not be able to take another "To do" card.

 d. **Waiting For** (Waiting):
  
 This column is optional, here we usually include the tasks that we think we have finished but do not depend on us (For example, if we send a design to a client we have to wait for the OK before moving it to "Done"). This helps us avoid unpleasant surprises and a backlog of work.

 and. **Done** (Done):
  
 Once the task is completed we move it to this column. Usually all cards in this column are archived at the end of the week. In our case, we will wait for the end of the course to be able to view all the exercises that we have done together.

 **2** . Add the tasks and different exercises on cards:
  
 Each card must be explanatory, we can assign a date and an estimate of hours.

 **Recommendations:**
  
 The Kanban board is a great project visualization tool that helps us “unload” our mind from pending tasks and easily and effectively manage the project process. If you don't use it yet, I highly recommend that you start using it to organize your work and projects.
- ### Do the Heuristic Evaluation (EH) 
Practice exercise 2\.1

The Heuristic Evaluation (EH) helps us to examine the usability of an interface based on compliance with the recognized principles of usability: heuristics.

 Evaluate the web [renfe.es](https://www.renfe.com/es/es) or another web that is well known with the [Heuristic Evaluation Template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1r4YqacoosBK4UeRqwBviBWLMh9ydDjkVu-8UyMGi7_U/edit?usp=sharing) that we use in Elastic Heads.

 Instructions:
  
 1\. Make a copy of the Template ("File" / "Make a copy")
  
 2\. Fill in the fields following the instructions.
  
 3\. Compare the result with your colleagues in the forum, for this you can share the resulting graph (lower tab "Graph") and briefly explain which are the most important usability "flaws" that you have found.
