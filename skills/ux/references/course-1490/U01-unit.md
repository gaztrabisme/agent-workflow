# Unit 1 · Introduction

Anchor: 1490 · U1

Hi! I am Óscar SP, welcome to my Domestika course.

 I am a professor of product design and user experience and with this course I want you to learn the different tools and processes that we use in a studio when conceptualizing a digital product.

 Let's get started!

## Tasks

Practice exercise 1\.2

Explain what idea you have come up with and what need or problem it would solve. If there are already similar ideas or products, no problem, it's a good sign;) If you can't think of what to do with your project, comment on it here and we will all try to help you.

## Additional resources

- ### The Elastic Heads Study

[Elastic Heads](http://elasticheads.com/)
  
 I present you our study so you can see the services we provide and write to us whenever you want.
- ### Videos

[*Simplicity sells*](https://www.ted.com/talks/david_pogue_simplicity_sells/up-next)
  
 Simplicity sells. The worst user interfaces explained by journalist David Pogue (and in this humorous video).

 [*Scrum: How to do twice as much in half the time*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s4thQcgLCqk)
  
 Super inspirational Jeff Sutherland TED talk on how agile methodologies and teamwork make us more productive.

 [*Lean UX: Getting Out of the Deliverables Business*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nvAEwXPqM3Y)
  
 Jeff Gothelf explains why we need to ditch UX design deliverables to be more effective.
- ### books

[*Steal like an artist*](https://www.amazon.es/dp/6071120853/ref=cm_sw_r_tw_dp_x_FZ.fFbJ54ZZH5)
  
 Ten tips on creativity rather on life, talks about copying like an artist, varying the references and improving the above, what we could call “Stealing with a white glove”.

 [*Lean vs. Agile vs. Design Thinking*](https://www.amazon.es/dp/B072KG93QJ/ref=cm_sw_r_tw_dp_x_C2.fFbKYQPF6E)
  
 Jeff Gothelf explains the three terms with advice and very good examples. Perfect as a first contact. It is also very very short.

 [*The psychology of everyday objects*](https://www.amazon.es/dp/B07D8X6JRM/ref=cm_sw_r_tw_dp_x_u3.fFb9HV014)
  
 The great Donald Norman classic that talks about the problems caused by poorly designed products that we use every day. (Be careful when buying! The copy of the Spanish edition is more than € 150\.)

 [*The laws of simplicity*](https://www.amazon.es/dp/8497845439/ref=cm_sw_r_tw_dp_x_i6.fFbQAZK3N)
  
 John Maeda explains in his book the advantages of simplifying design, technology, business and life through ten laws explained with examples. It is a bit metaphysical but easy to read.

 [*Lean UX*](https://www.amazon.es/dp/8416125023/ref=cm_sw_r_tw_dp_x_Z6.fFb71960E2)
  
 Jeff Gothelf teaches us a (not so) new way of working in UX applying Lean Startup principles, Agile and Design Thinking exercises based on the scientific method.
- ### Social network of new products and services

[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/)
  
 This is a social network to publish and vote for new products and services creating communication between the public and the creators. Perfect to inspire us, get ideas and see trends in product and *gadgets* and also in how the web pages of new companies are designed.
