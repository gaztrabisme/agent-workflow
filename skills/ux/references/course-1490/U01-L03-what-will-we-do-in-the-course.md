## Moving from an idea to a prototype

Anchor: 1490 · U1 · L03 · 00:00–00:31

In this course, we will learn the techniques and tools needed to take an idea through to a prototype. We can use this prototype to test with real users and improve our value proposition (*propuesta de valor*). We will learn why a good process matters when designing a product, whether analog or digital. We will optimize that process so we design only what is needed to add value.

## Designing with users in mind

Anchor: 1490 · U1 · L03 · 00:31–00:58

There will be no more designing three versions of the same website or making endless changes to a proposal. As I did in the past, many designers do not take users into account in their work. They create many attractive designs for their portfolios, but do not consider usability or who will use those products. With this course, I want to teach you the methodology and tools needed to design with users and usability in mind. We will learn to shape our ideas by considering users, their needs and even their fears.

## Practical research and usability

Anchor: 1490 · U1 · L03 · 00:58–01:21

Contrary to what large consultancies may tell you, researching users does not have to be expensive or difficult. We will learn the techniques we use in studios such as Elastic Heads (captions: *ActiveHeads*) and that Silicon Valley startups use. We will discover the main usability heuristics (*heurísticas de usabilidad*) that will help make our products easy to use and, in turn, improve the user experience (*experiencia de usuario*).

## Hand prototyping and iteration

Anchor: 1490 · U1 · L03 · 01:21–01:44

We will learn to design and prototype by hand so we can make changes as early as possible and iterate on our design. The exercises and tools we will use are very easy to use. Now that you know everything we will cover in the course, in the next lesson we will begin with an introduction to user-centered design (*diseño centrado en el usuario*).
