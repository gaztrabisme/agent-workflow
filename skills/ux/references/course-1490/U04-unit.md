# Unit 4 · Empathize: User-Centered Design

Anchor: 1490 · U4

In this unit I will explain the most important exercises so that we can empathize with users.

## Tasks

- ### Create proto\-people 
Practice exercise 4\.1

The *user personas* identify the characters in our user stories and, by giving them a name and a personality, it is easier for us to empathize with the group of users they represent. With this we will offer solutions that are more appropriate to your needs.

 It has to collect the most important concepts of our character with respect to our product.

 Make two proto\-people for the main audiences of your project taking into account the information from the research.

 To make a proto\-person we fold a sheet in four and in each of the four areas we include.

 1\. **Name and descriptive nickname** : A quick way to identify and recognize the "person". (Here we usually draw the character or include a photo).

 2\. **Demographic data** : Helps to classify the character within a market segment.

 3\. **Behavior** : How does this user behave with respect to the activity of our project? What is this person like?

 4\. **Goals and Objectives** : We look for the needs of the user that he has regarding our project.

- ### Create an empathy map 
Practice exercise 4\.2

The empathy map will help us to get inside the head of our "person".

 1\. **Who are we going to empathize with?** include the data of one of our user persona.

 2\. **What do you need to do?** What actions do you need to take regarding our product or service? (We have it in the needs section of the Proto\-person)

 3\. **What do you see?** What do you see in the market? What do you see others doing? What do you see around you?

 4\. **What does it say?** What do we imagine it can say?

 5\. **What does it do?** What behaviors do you usually do?

 6\. **What do you hear?** What do you hear from your friends, family, or co\-workers?

 7\. **Pains and benefits** . Here we will include both the obstacles and challenges that you may encounter when using our product and the benefits that we could bring you.

- ### Create a proto\-journey 
Practice exercise 4\.3

1\. Based on a *proto\-person* we decide what action to take on our product. (For example add a book or share a review in the example of the Reading App).

 2\. We propose each step that the user performs (from 5 to 8\) and how they would feel in each of them placing each step higher or lower depending on how we think they feel.

 3\. Once the process is completed, we select the most relevant *paint points* and design opportunities 🔴 (From 3 to 5\).

 4\. We use the “How Might We…?” Technique. (How could we…?) To turn pain points into solutions.

 5\. We write down the ideas and functionalities for each of the HMWs.

 6\. If we are carrying out the project in a group we can vote for the most interesting ideas with  green stickers\[/i] 🟢

- ### Tools

[Figma](https://www.figma.com/)

 [Mural](https://www.mural.co/)
  
 It is a tool to perform Design Thinking exercises remotely designed for non\-designers so it is very easy to use. The tool itself has templates for the most important UX exercises.

 [Miro](https://miro.com/)
  
 Another tool to perform Design Thinking exercises remotely, very similar to Mural, but with better UX.
