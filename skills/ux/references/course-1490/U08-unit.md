# Unit 8 · Success stories and final tips

Anchor: 1490 · U8

We have reached the last unit of the course!

 As product designers we have to be able to explain the process and why of the decisions we have made, many times more important than the simple design proposal, and here we will do it through a success story.

 We will also review the course and give you some final tips so that you can continue learning.

## Tasks

- ### Write your success story in UX 
Practice exercise 8\.1

Now that we have our project, it is time to write the success story explaining the work process and what you have learned from the project.

 Remember to structure the speech as we have seen in the video of the lesson:
