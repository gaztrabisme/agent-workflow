## Start with a product or service

Anchor: 798 · U1 · L04 · 00:00–00:35

This exercise is meant to get you writing and help you find your rhythm. We will start working on the first exercise for your final project. The idea is to use a client you already have, your personal brand, or a hobby you like, and gradually make screens for a product or service. First, write an “About me” text for that product or service. Before you start writing, there are many questions to consider.

## Answer the first question in Figma

Anchor: 798 · U1 · L04 · 00:35–01:11

To simplify things, I will give you three questions I would like you to answer. The first is: what does your product or service do? To answer it, go to the T here, or press the T key on your keyboard. You can create a text block (*bloque de texto*) here and put in your answer. You can change the text color to whichever you like. If you prefer, for example... Here is one. Or how about white? Whatever you want.

## Answer the remaining questions and structure the text

Anchor: 798 · U1 · L04 · 01:11–01:51

The next question is: what benefit does it provide? You can return to that text block or create another one, or you can copy and paste (captions: *¿Piar? y pegar*). The third question is: what makes it different from other products or services? Once you have written your answers to those three questions, look them over. Then start structuring the “About me” text that you can use in the About Me section for your product or service. The idea is to do it here in Figma so you can start practicing.

## Practice with basic wireframes

Anchor: 798 · U1 · L04 · 01:51–02:24

At first, the tool may be a little difficult. If you come from another job where you used tools such as Photoshop or Sketch, they tend to be similar. But do not worry; we will go step by step, with a little patience. The idea is to practice. You do not need to make anything visually complex here, just basic wireframes (*wireframes*) that help you get familiar with the tool and gradually learn. So, keep going—you can do it.

### Key points (derived)

- Choose a real client, personal brand, or hobby and draft an “About me” text for it.
- Answer what the product does, what benefit it offers, and what makes it different before structuring the text.
- Use basic Figma wireframes to practice; the visual design can stay simple.
