# Unit 2 · Introduction to UX Writing

Anchor: 798 · U2

In this unit I am going to explain what a UX *writer* , what their role and responsibilities are, and I will teach you what it means to be part of the product team.

## Tasks

- ### Learn more about Figma 
Practice exercise 2\.1

Create two *frames* in Figma. In one, you will define what problem your product or service solves (client, *hobby* or personal brand) and in another, how your App solves that problem.

 Do this exercise in a *frame* of Figma so you can share it later.
- ### Fill in the proto\-persona canvas

Based on the information you have available through research on the type of person who will use your App, fill in the proto\-person canvas that will help you have a better idea who you are writing for.

 Do this exercise in an *frame* of [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resources/assets/user-persona-template/) so you can share it later.

[*Writing Is Designing*](https://rosenfeldmedia.com/books/writing-is-designing/)
