# Unit 3 · Basic principles

Anchor: 798 · U3

We are going to review what are the basic principles that you will need to dedicate yourself to UX Writing. From how design depends on words, understanding the science behind how we read and why we say that digital systems are like conversation.

## Tasks

- ### Adapt this message

Clear, concise and useful. Starting from the original message:

 **Error**
  
 **An authentication error occurred**
  
 **OK**

 Adapt this error message to your product or service so that you can use it in your client's App or *hobby* or personal brand. Make it clearer first. After clear and also concise. Finally try again and now also make it useful. Do this exercise in a *frame* of Figma so you can share it later.
- ### This, but not this: create your list 
Practice exercise 3\.1

With your client, *hobby* or "persona brand" I will ask you to create a list of at least 10 concepts with the format "This, but not this."

 Do this exercise in a *frame* of Figma so you can share it later.
- ### Design a conversation 
Practice exercise 3\.2

With the product or service you chose and based on your "This, but not this" list. Write a WhatsApp or Messenger style conversation in which the system welcomes and gives a brief summary of what the user can do when using the product or service.

 Do this exercise in a *frame* of Figma so you can share it later.

## Additional resources

- ### Books in English

 [*Conversational Design* , Erika Hall](https://abookapart.com/products/conversational-design)
  
 Talk about how to make digital systems more human through conversation design thinking. Chapter 3 explains in more detail how to put the principles of this type of design into practice.

 [*Strategic Writing for UX* , Torrey Podmajerski](http://shop.oreilly.com/product/0636920235583.do)
  
 Very practical for people who want to start UX Writing. Chapter 5 talks more in detail about how to edit your texts keeping in mind the principles of clear, concise and useful.

 [*Microcopy The Complete Guide* , Kinneret Yifrah](https://www.microcopybook.com/)
  
 With very practical examples of different types of *microcopy* and how they are used in various interfaces. Part 3 talks more in detail about accessibility.

 [Mailchimp voice and tone guide](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/)
  
 With very practical examples of different types of *microcopy* and how they are used in various interfaces. Part 3 talks more in detail about accessibility.

 [*Nicely Said* , Kate Kiefer Lee and Nicole Fenton](http://www.nicelysaid.co/)
  
 On how to write for the web and digital products. In Chapter 5 he talks about the “this, but not this” tool.
- ### Reading patterns in F

["F\-Shaped Pattern of Reading on the Web: Misunderstood, But Still Relevant (Even on Mobile)"](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content/)
  
 This article goes into more detail about reading patterns and how users consume content on screens.
