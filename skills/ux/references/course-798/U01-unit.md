# Unit 1 · Introduction

Anchor: 798 · U1

Hello! Welcome to my Domestika course. In this unit I am going to tell you a little about myself, what I do and I will share with you some of my projects that I have done as a UX *writer* . I will also tell you a bit about my influences, where I get inspiration from and I will recommend some books that I have found useful throughout my career.

## Tasks

- ### Share your references 
Practice exercise 1\.2

And what inspires you?
  
 Share your references, be they books, talks, interviews or articles that you find useful and inspiring about UX Writing.
- ### Write your text About us

Write a short description about your product or service (client, hobby or personal brand) based on the lesson " *Warm\-up* ". Start by writing the following questions on a blank page in Figma and try to answer them as briefly as possible:

 What does your product or service do?
  
 What benefit (s) does it give to users?
  
 What problem are you trying to solve?
  
 What makes it different from other products or services that are dedicated to the same?

 Once you have the answers to those questions, structure your text as if it were an “About us” section and this text will serve as an introduction.
