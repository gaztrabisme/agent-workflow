# Unit 4 · Write for user experience

Anchor: 798 · U4

Let's do it! Or rather fingers on the keyboard. In this unit I am going to explain different types of texts or *microcopy* that are present in an App and I will give you some recommendations on how to approach, create and iterate them.

## Tasks

- ### Write the microcopy of various interactions 
Practice exercise 4\.1

With the product or service you chose and based on your “this, but not this” list and the homework conversation from the last unit. Write the following types of messages for your App:

 *Onboarding*
  
 1 screen without content
  
 3 error messages for 3 different situations
  
 1 notification *push*
  
 1 App alert
  
 1 account creation screen

 Do this exercise in several *frames* of Figma so you can share it later.

## Additional resources

- ### Articles I Recommend

["How to test content"](https://medium.com/think-by-shifta/c%C3%B3mo-testear-el-contenido-1a6327428fc3)
  
 Helena Feliu gives us some keys to what you can do to be able to test your content.

 ["Plan before writing in UX writing"](https://www.lauxcritora.com/ux-writing/planear-antes-de-escribir-en-ux-writing/)
  
 Blanca Rego gives us techniques on how you can be more clear about what your user is looking for.

 ["Consistency in UX writing"](https://www.lauxcritora.com/ux-writing/consistencia-ux-writing/)
  
 Blanca Rego explains to us in this article how to make sure we maintain consistency in UX Writing.

 ["Stop giving me orders and write a CTA that encourages me to act"](https://medium.com/@Luciferia/deja-de-darme-%C3%B3rdenes-y-escribe-un-cta-que-me-anime-a-actuar-3ba223139944)
  
 Lucía Alcayde gives several tips on what to keep in mind when writing a good call to action.

 ["Anatomy of a good message"](https://medium.com/narrative-ux/anatomia-de-un-buen-mensaje-f2da0ff78782)
  
 Iván Leal does a great analysis of the things that must be taken into account to write a good message for a digital product or service.

 ["Why does Big Data need Thick Data?"](https://medium.com/@triciawang/por-qu%C3%A9-big-data-necesita-thick-data-fbbe11dfb088)
  
 Tricia Wang explains in this article what she means by both types of data and gives us examples and explanations of how one needs the other.

 ["How many users do I need for a usability test?"](https://blog.ida.cl/experiencia-de-usuario/usuarios-necesarios-test-usabilidad/)
  
 This IDA blog post explains in detail what it was saying about Nielsen's study of how many users you need to start finding patterns.
