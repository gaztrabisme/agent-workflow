# First read
- Is the most important element the first thing the eye lands on?
- Can a reader state the slide's main point after one quick look?
- Is the title clear and easy to find?
# Reading and meaning
- Is the text comfortable to read at the intended presentation size?
- Do the visual hierarchy and spacing make the content easy to scan?
- Do the visuals support the slide's main point?
- Are colour and labels sufficient to understand the meaning?
- Does the slide feel balanced, with no distracting empty or crowded area?
