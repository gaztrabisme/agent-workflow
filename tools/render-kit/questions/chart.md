# First read
- Is the main data pattern the first thing the eye lands on?
- Can a reader identify the chart's main takeaway without guessing?
- Are the axes and units clear enough to interpret the values?
- Are labels readable at the size in which this chart will be viewed?
# Comparison and detail
- Are the series distinguishable without relying on colour alone?
- Does each mark appear to match its stated value and category?
- Is the amount of detail appropriate for the question this chart answers?
- Can a reader trace the important comparisons without losing their place?
